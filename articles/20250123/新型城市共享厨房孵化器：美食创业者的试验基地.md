#### 1.1 Background and Definition of New Urban Shared Kitchen Incubators

**1.1.1 Problem Background**
- **Description:** The traditional restaurant industry faces high entry barriers due to high initial investment costs, stringent regulatory requirements, and the challenges of maintaining consistent quality and service.
- **Solutions:** Shared kitchen incubators offer a cost-effective alternative, providing entrepreneurs with access to well-equipped facilities and reducing their operational risks.

**1.1.2 Definition of New Urban Shared Kitchen Incubators**
- **Conceptual Terms:** New Urban Shared Kitchen Incubators are shared-use facilities designed to support culinary entrepreneurs by providing them with access to professional-grade kitchen equipment, utilities, and a collaborative working environment.
- **Characteristics:** These incubators are typically located in urban areas, focusing on providing an environment conducive to food business development.

**1.1.3 Importance and Challenges**
- **Importance:** They facilitate innovation in the food industry, reduce environmental impact, and contribute to the diversification of local food options.
- **Challenges:** They face regulatory hurdles, need to balance diverse tenant requirements, and must ensure hygiene and safety standards.

#### 1.2 Characteristics and Types of New Urban Shared Kitchen Incubators

**1.2.1 Main Characteristics**
- **Shared Resources:** Entrepreneurs share kitchen space, equipment, and utilities, reducing individual costs.
- **Flexibility:** Shared kitchen incubators offer flexible lease terms, allowing businesses to scale up or down as needed.
- **Professional Support:** Many incubators provide business consulting, marketing assistance, and networking opportunities.

**1.2.2 Types and Categories**
- **By Scale:** Small-scale incubators focusing on individual entrepreneurs, mid-scale incubators serving multiple startups, and large-scale facilities accommodating multiple food businesses.
- **By Specialization:** Incubators dedicated to specific cuisines or food categories (e.g., vegan, fast-casual), and those offering niche services like event catering or educational programs.
