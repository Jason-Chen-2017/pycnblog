## 1.2 Core Concepts and Relationships

### 1.2.1 Self-Consistency (Self-Consistency)

#### Definition
Self-consistency refers to the property of an AI system's response where the generated answer is logically coherent and consistent with the given question and the system's internal knowledge base.

#### Characteristics
- Logical coherence: The response must be free from contradictions and should logically follow from the given question and available information.
- Internal consistency: The response should align with the system's internal representations of knowledge and beliefs.

#### Distinctions from Other Related Concepts

Self-consistency is often confused with consistency in machine learning models. While both concepts aim to ensure coherence, self-consistency is more focused on the internal logic and coherence of the AI system's responses, rather than the consistency of the model's predictions.

### 1.2.2 Common Sense Theory of Mind (CoT)

#### Definition
Common Sense Theory of Mind (CoT) is a cognitive model that enables AI systems to understand and reason about the mental states and actions of others, based on their observable behaviors and contextual information.

#### Functions
- Contextual understanding: CoT helps AI systems to interpret the context of a question and generate responses that are relevant and appropriate.
- Social intelligence: CoT enables AI systems to exhibit social intelligence by understanding and responding to human emotions and social cues.

#### Relationship with Self-Consistency

CoT and self-consistency are closely related. While CoT focuses on understanding the context and generating contextually appropriate responses, self-consistency ensures that these responses are logically coherent and consistent with the system's internal knowledge base.

### 1.3.1 Mathematical Model of Self-Consistency

#### Model Introduction
The mathematical model of self-consistency is designed to ensure that the AI system's responses are logically coherent. It uses a set of rules and constraints to verify the consistency of the generated responses.

#### Mathematical Formulas

Let R be the set of responses generated by the AI system, Q be the set of questions, KB be the knowledge base, and C be the context.

The self-consistency check can be defined as:

$$
\forall r \in R, \forall q \in Q, \forall c \in C, (r \text{ is consistent with } q \text{ and } KB) \iff \neg \exists x, (r \text{ contradicts } x \text{ from } KB)
$$

This formula states that a response r is consistent with a question q and the knowledge base KB if there is no contradiction between r and any other information x in KB.
