### 1. Introduction and Overview

#### 1.1. Book Background

In the era of big data, the ability to visualize high-dimensional data has become increasingly critical. Traditional data visualization techniques often fall short when dealing with data that contains hundreds or even thousands of dimensions. This limitation has led to the emergence of advanced methods, such as AI-assisted data visualization, to address the challenges posed by high-dimensional data.

Zero-Shot Learning (ZSL) is an innovative approach in machine learning that enables models to learn and make predictions without any training data. ZSL has shown great promise in various domains, including image recognition, natural language processing, and, more recently, data visualization. The integration of ZSL with AI-assisted data visualization represents a significant breakthrough in the field, offering new opportunities for analyzing and understanding complex datasets.

This book aims to provide a comprehensive overview of Zero-Shot Learning in AI-assisted high-dimensional data visualization. We will explore the core concepts, methodologies, and algorithms that enable ZSL, discuss the challenges and opportunities in high-dimensional data visualization, and provide practical insights and examples. By the end of the book, readers will have a deep understanding of ZSL's potential to revolutionize the field of data visualization, equipping them with the knowledge and skills to apply these techniques in various domains.
