## Keywords: Formalization, Prompt Language, Natural Language Processing, Tool Development, Syntax, Semantics, Formal Methods

## Abstract:  
The aim of this book is to explore the development of formalization tools for prompt language specification, focusing on the integration of formal methods in natural language processing. We delve into the fundamentals of formalization, the characteristics of prompt languages, and the challenges in their processing. The book presents a systematic approach to designing and implementing formalization tools, accompanied by practical applications and case studies. Through a step-by-step analysis, we aim to provide a comprehensive guide for researchers and practitioners in the field of artificial intelligence and natural language processing.
