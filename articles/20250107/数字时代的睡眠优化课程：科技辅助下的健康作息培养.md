### Chapter 2: Sleep Science Basics

#### 2.1 Types and Cycles of Sleep

Sleep is divided into two main types: Rapid Eye Movement (REM) sleep and Non-Rapid Eye Movement (NREM) sleep. The sleep cycle alternates between these phases, each serving different functions in the restoration and regulation of the body.

#### 2.2 Sleep Disorders and Symptoms

Common sleep disorders include insomnia, sleep apnea, and hypersomnia. Each disorder manifests with specific symptoms that can significantly impact a person's daily functioning.

#### 2.3 Physiological Mechanisms of Sleep

The sleep-wake cycle is regulated by the body's internal clock, known as the circadian rhythm. Hormonal changes, such as the release of melatonin, play a crucial role in regulating sleep.
