The book title "From Classical AI to Deep Learning: Paradigm Shift in Machine Understanding of the World" indicates a transformation from the traditional methods of AI to a new paradigm driven by deep learning. - **Deep Learning**: A subset of machine learning concerned with neural networks that learn from large amounts of data.
- **Paradigm Shift**: A fundamental change in how concepts are understood or problems are approached.
- **Machine Understanding**: The capability of machines to perceive and interpret the world in a manner similar to humans.
