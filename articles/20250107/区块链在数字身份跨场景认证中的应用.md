### Part 1: Introduction to Blockchain and Digital Identity

#### 1.1 Background and Overview

**1.1.1 The Rise of Blockchain Technology**

Blockchain technology has evolved significantly since its inception in 2008 with the launch of Bitcoin. This decentralized, distributed ledger system offers a secure and transparent way to record transactions without the need for a central authority. Let's delve into the origins and key principles of blockchain:

- **Origin and Evolution**: Blockchain was created by an anonymous person or group of people using the pseudonym Satoshi Nakamoto. Its inception was driven by the need for a transparent and tamper-proof transaction system.
- **Fundamental Principles**: Blockchain operates on three core principles: decentralization, distributed consensus, and cryptographic security.

**1.1.2 The Concept and Importance of Digital Identity**

Digital identity refers to the representation of a person or entity in the digital world. It encompasses various attributes and information that uniquely identify an individual or organization. Traditional digital identity management systems face several challenges:

- **Definition of Digital Identity**: Digital identity includes information such as personal details, biometrics, and digital credentials.
- **Challenges in Traditional Management**: Issues like data breaches, identity theft, and the need for multiple credentials for different services highlight the need for a more robust system.

**1.1.3 The Intersection of Blockchain and Digital Identity**

Blockchain technology offers several advantages that can address the challenges in digital identity management:

- **Potential in Digital Identity Management**: Blockchain can provide a secure and tamper-proof method for storing and managing digital identities.
- **Overview of Cross-scene Authentication**: Cross-scene authentication refers to the ability to verify a user's identity across different platforms and services. Blockchain enables this by ensuring a seamless and secure authentication process.
