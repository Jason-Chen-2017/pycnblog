                 


### Introduction

#### 1.1 Research Background

In today's interconnected world, the understanding and analysis of complex systems have become increasingly important. The field of computer science and engineering has seen significant advancements, from the early days of General Systems Theory to the modern era of Complex Network Theory. This article aims to explore the transition between these two theories and their implications for understanding our highly interconnected world.

**Problem Background:**

The development of General Systems Theory began in the mid-20th century, inspired by the need to understand the behavior of complex systems in various fields such as biology, physics, and sociology. This theory provided a framework for studying systems as a whole, emphasizing the interrelationships between their components. However, as systems became more complex and interconnected, the limitations of General Systems Theory became evident. This led to the development of Complex Systems Theory, which addresses the challenges posed by the increased complexity and interdependence of modern systems.

**Problem Description:**

The problem we aim to address in this article is the transition from General Systems Theory to Complex Network Theory. While General Systems Theory provides a foundational understanding of systems, it has limitations in handling highly interconnected systems. Complex Network Theory offers a more sophisticated approach to studying these systems, enabling us to better understand their behavior, dynamics, and applications.

**Problem Solution:**

The solution to this problem involves exploring the fundamental concepts and principles of both General Systems Theory and Complex Network Theory. We will examine their similarities, differences, and applications in various fields. By doing so, we can gain a deeper understanding of how these theories can be used to analyze and model complex, highly interconnected systems.

**Boundaries and Extensions:**

The scope of this article is limited to the theoretical aspects of General Systems Theory and Complex Network Theory. We will not delve into specific applications or case studies, but rather provide a comprehensive overview of the core concepts and principles. This article aims to serve as a foundation for further exploration and research in this field.

#### 1.2 Research Significance

**Disciplinary Development Needs:**

The study of General Systems Theory and Complex Network Theory is crucial for advancing our understanding of complex systems. These theories have been used to model and analyze a wide range of systems, from biological networks to social networks and technological systems. The practical value of these theories lies in their ability to provide insights into the behavior and dynamics of complex systems. By understanding the principles of General Systems Theory and Complex Network Theory, we can develop more effective strategies for addressing real-world challenges and creating innovative solutions.
