### Core Concepts and Relationships

#### Definition of Graph Theory

A graph consists of nodes (also called vertices) and edges that connect these nodes. There are different types of graphs, including directed and undirected graphs, weighted and unweighted graphs. Graphs can be represented in various ways, such as adjacency matrix, adjacency list, and incidence matrix.

#### Point of Laughter

A point of laughter is a specific moment or element in a piece of humor that is intended to evoke laughter from the audience. These points can vary widely in nature, from clever wordplay to absurd situations.

from collections import defaultdict

# Create a graph representation of the joke

# Calculate the degree of each node
