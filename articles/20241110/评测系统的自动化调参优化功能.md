### Introduction

#### Background

Evaluation systems are an essential component of modern machine learning and artificial intelligence applications. These systems are designed to assess the performance of algorithms in various domains, such as image recognition, natural language processing, and reinforcement learning. The primary goal of an evaluation system is to provide a quantitative measure of the algorithm's effectiveness, which can then be used to compare different algorithms, identify areas for improvement, and optimize the overall system performance.

The performance of an algorithm is heavily dependent on the choice of parameters. Parameters are adjustable settings that influence the behavior of the algorithm. For example, in a machine learning model, parameters such as learning rate, regularization strength, and network architecture can significantly impact the model's performance. However, finding the optimal parameter settings is a challenging task due to the large search space and the potential for overfitting or underfitting.
