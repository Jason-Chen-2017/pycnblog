### 1.1 Problem Background

Software reliability engineering is an essential discipline within the field of software engineering. The primary goal of software reliability engineering is to ensure that software systems function correctly under specified conditions for the required period of time. The importance of software reliability cannot be overstated, as the reliability of software systems directly impacts their users' satisfaction, trust, and the overall success of a business.

**What is software reliability?** Simply put, software reliability is the probability that a software system will perform its intended functions without failure over a specified period of time under given conditions. A software system that is reliable will meet its specified requirements and perform its functions correctly under normal, abnormal, and extreme conditions.

**Challenges in Building High-Availability Systems**

Building high-availability systems presents numerous challenges. Some of these challenges include:

1. **Complexity**: Modern software systems are highly complex, with numerous components, interactions, and dependencies. Ensuring the reliability of such systems is a daunting task.
2. **Scalability**: As software systems grow in size and complexity, they must also be scalable to handle increasing workloads. Ensuring reliability while scaling can be challenging.
