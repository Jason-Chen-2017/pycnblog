#### 1.2 Fundamental Knowledge of Artificial Intelligence

**AI Definition and Development:**
- AI refers to the simulation of human intelligence in machines that are programmed to think like humans and mimic their actions.
- The development of AI has a rich history, from early concepts in philosophy and logic to the advent of modern computing and machine learning.

**Core AI Technologies:**
- **Machine Learning:** A subset of AI that focuses on developing algorithms that enable computers to learn from and make predictions or decisions based on data.
- **Deep Learning:** A subfield of machine learning that uses neural networks with many layers to extract high-level features from raw data.
- **Natural Language Processing (NLP):** An area of AI focused on enabling computers to understand, interpret, and generate human language.
- **Computer Vision:** A field of AI that enables computers to interpret and understand the world in terms of images, videos, and other visual inputs.

### Chapter 3: Core Concepts in Mental Health Intervention

#### 3.1 Definition and Criteria of Mental Health

**Mental Health Definition:**
- Mental health refers to a state of well-being in which individuals can realize their own abilities, cope with normal stressors of life, work productively, and contribute to their community.

**Criteria for Mental Health:**
- Psychological well-being
- Social functioning
- Emotional stability
- Cognitive abilities
- Adaptability to change

**Differences between Mental Health and Mental Illness:**
- Mental health is a continuum, and everyone experiences different levels of well-being at different times.
- Mental illness refers to conditions that significantly impair an individual's ability to function in daily life.

#### 3.2 Concept and Characteristics of Personalized Intervention

**Personalized Intervention Definition:**
- Personalized intervention is an approach that tailors mental health treatment to the unique needs, characteristics, and preferences of each individual.

**Characteristics of Personalized Intervention:**
- Individualization of treatment plans
- Tailored therapeutic techniques
- Continuous monitoring and feedback
- Adaptive and responsive to patient progress

**Challenges and Opportunities:**
- **Challenges:**
  - Accurate assessment of individual differences
  - Complexity of integrating diverse data sources
  - Ensuring ethical use of patient data
- **Opportunities:**
  - Improved patient outcomes
  - Increased access to mental health care
  - Enhanced efficiency and scalability of treatment delivery
