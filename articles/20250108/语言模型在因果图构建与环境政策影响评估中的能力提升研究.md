                 



### Introduction and Structure of the Book

#### Title: The Ability Enhancement of Language Models in Causal Graph Construction and Environmental Policy Impact Assessment

#### Keywords:
- Language Models
- Causal Graphs
- Environmental Policy
- Impact Assessment
- Artificial Intelligence

#### Abstract:
This book delves into the integration of advanced language models with causal graph construction for enhanced environmental policy impact assessment. It provides a comprehensive overview of the theoretical foundations, algorithmic advancements, and practical applications in the field. By combining principles from natural language processing, causal inference, and environmental science, the book offers a unique perspective on how AI can be harnessed to inform and improve environmental policies.
