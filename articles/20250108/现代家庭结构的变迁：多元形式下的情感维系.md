1. **信任指数**：\( T = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} t_i \)

2. **沟通效率**：\( E = \frac{M}{N} \)

3. **共情指数**：\( E = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} e_i \)

4. **支持指数**：\( S = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} s_i \)
