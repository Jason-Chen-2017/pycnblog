### 4.1 评估模型的数学基础

#### 4.1.1 研发管线质量指数的计算公式
$$ Q = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n} w_i \cdot S_i}{\sum_{i=1}^{n} w_i} $$
其中，\( Q \) 表示研发管线质量指数，\( w_i \) 表示第 \( i \) 个评估维度的权重，\( S_i \) 表示第 \( i \) 个维度的评分。

#### 4.1.2 权重分配的依据与方法

```python
def calculate_quality_index(projects):
    weights = [0.4, 0.3, 0.2, 0.1]
    dimensions = ['innovation', 'feasibility', 'market_potential', 'risk']
    quality_index = {}
    for project in projects:
        scores = {}
        for i in range(len(dimensions)):
            scores[dimensions[i]] = project[i]
        weighted_sum = sum(weights[i] * scores[dimensions[i]] for i in range(len(weights)))
        total_weight = sum(weights)
        qi = weighted_sum / total_weight
        quality_index[project['name']] = qi
    return quality_index
```
