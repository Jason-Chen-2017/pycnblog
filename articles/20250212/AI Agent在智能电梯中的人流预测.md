#### 3.3 数学模型与公式
- **ARIMA模型公式**
  $$
  ARIMA(p, d, q)：X_t = \phi_1 X_{t-1} + \dots + \phi_p X_{t-p} + \theta_1 \epsilon_{t-1} + \dots + \theta_q \epsilon_{t-q} + \epsilon_t
  $$
- **LSTM网络结构**
  $$
  i_t = \sigma(W_i h_{t-1} + U_i x_t)
  $$
  $$
  f_t = \sigma(W_f h_{t-1} + U_f x_t)
  $$
  $$
  o_t = \sigma(W_o h_{t-1} + U_o x_t)
  $$
  $$
  g_t = \tanh(W_g h_{t-1} + U_g x_t)
  $$
  $$
  h_t = i_t \cdot g_t + f_t \cdot h_{t-1}
  $$
