### Book Overview

**Title:** AI in Multi-Objective Optimization for Inter-basin Water Resources Allocation and Ecological Water Supplementation

**Keywords:** AI, Water Resources Management, Multi-Objective Optimization, Inter-basin Allocation, Ecological Water Supplementation

**Abstract:**
This book delves into the application of artificial intelligence (AI) in solving complex problems of water resources allocation and ecological water supplementation across different river basins. It explores the principles and methodologies of multi-objective optimization using AI to balance various objectives such as water availability, ecological health, and socio-economic needs. The book aims to provide a comprehensive guide to understanding the role of AI in enhancing the efficiency and sustainability of water resource management.
