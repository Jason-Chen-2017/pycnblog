## Key Concepts and Terminology

### Zero-Shot Learning

Zero-shot learning (ZSL) refers to the ability of a machine learning model to classify or predict outcomes for classes it has never seen during training. In other words, the model can generalize to new classes without requiring any labeled examples of those classes.

### GPT-3

GPT-3, or the Generative Pre-trained Transformer 3, is a language model developed by OpenAI. It is known for its vast vocabulary and the ability to generate human-like text based on the input it receives. GPT-3's zero-shot learning capability is particularly noteworthy due to its unparalleled performance in various NLP tasks without the need for task-specific fine-tuning.
