### Introduction to the Topic

#### Background and Core Concepts

**评测系统（Evaluation System）** refers to a computer-based system designed to assess, measure, and evaluate various attributes or performance metrics. It can be used in diverse fields such as education, quality control, healthcare, and more. Key components of an evaluation system include data collection, data processing, model training, and model evaluation.

**神经计算机（Neural Computer）** is an advanced computational model inspired by the human brain's neural networks. It's capable of processing and learning from data in ways that traditional computers cannot. The core advantage of neural computers lies in their ability to handle complex patterns and relationships within data.

**可微分神经计算机（Differentiable Neural Computer）** extends the capabilities of traditional neural computers by allowing them to be trained using gradient-based optimization techniques. This makes it possible to integrate neural computers into larger systems and apply them to a wide range of tasks, including evaluation systems.

### Chapter 4: Differentiable Neural Computers

**4.1 Definition of Differentiable Neural Computers**

Differentiable neural computers extend the capabilities of traditional neural computers by incorporating gradient-based optimization techniques. This allows them to be trained more effectively and integrated into larger systems.

**4.2 Working Principles**

The working principle of differentiable neural computers involves three main stages: input processing, computation, and output generation. During training, the computer adjusts its internal parameters to minimize the difference between its predictions and actual outcomes.

**4.3 Advantages of Differentiable Neural Computers**

Key advantages of differentiable neural computers include:
- Improved learning efficiency
- Enhanced accuracy in predictions
- Greater flexibility in handling various types of data
- Integration into larger, more complex systems
