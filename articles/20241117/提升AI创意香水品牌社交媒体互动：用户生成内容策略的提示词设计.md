#### 1.2 Social Media Platforms

Social media platforms have revolutionized the way brands interact with customers. They provide a platform for real-time communication, content sharing, and engagement. Some of the popular social media platforms include:

1. **Facebook**: A versatile platform for brand promotion, customer engagement, and advertising.
2. **Instagram**: A highly visual platform popular among young adults and influencers.
3. **Twitter**: A platform known for real-time updates and rapid information sharing.
4. **LinkedIn**: A professional networking platform suitable for B2B interactions.

**Mathematical Model and Explanation:**

The engagement rate on a social media platform can be calculated using the following formula:

$$
\text{Engagement Rate} = \frac{\text{Total Interactions}}{\text{Total Followers}} \times 100
$$

**Example:**

A brand has 100,000 followers and has received 10,000 interactions in the last month. The engagement rate would be:

$$
\text{Engagement Rate} = \frac{10,000}{100,000} \times 100 = 10\%
$$

#### 1.3 AI Applications in Social Media

AI has transformed social media by enabling personalized content delivery, automated customer service, and advanced analytics. Here are some key applications:

1. **Personalized Advertising**: AI algorithms can analyze user data to deliver personalized ads, increasing the chances of user engagement and conversion.
2. **Chatbots**: AI-powered chatbots can handle customer inquiries, provide product recommendations, and assist with transactions, improving customer experience.
3. **Content Curation**: AI can analyze user preferences and generate personalized content recommendations, enhancing user engagement.
4. **Sentiment Analysis**: AI can analyze social media posts and comments to gauge public opinion, helping brands to understand customer sentiment and respond accordingly.

**Mathematical Model and Explanation:**

The accuracy of sentiment analysis can be measured using the F1 score, which is the harmonic mean of precision and recall:

$$
F1\ score = \frac{2 \times precision \times recall}{precision + recall}
$$

**Example:**

A sentiment analysis model has a precision of 0.85 and a recall of 0.90. The F1 score would be:

$$
F1\ score = \frac{2 \times 0.85 \times 0.90}{0.85 + 0.90} = 0.87
$$
