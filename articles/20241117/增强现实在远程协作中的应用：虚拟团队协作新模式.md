**Enhanced Reality (ER)**, often mistaken for Virtual Reality (VR), is a more nuanced technology that enhances our perception of the real world with computer-generated sensory experiences, such as sound, video, haptic feedback, and more. ER is particularly impactful in remote collaboration, offering a more immersive and interactive experience than traditional video conferencing.

In the context of remote collaboration, ER provides a new model for virtual team collaboration by enabling team members to interact with a shared workspace, complete with virtual objects and information overlays, as if they were physically present in the same location. This is a significant evolution from static video calls, where interaction is limited and the sense of presence is often lacking.

The book aims to explore this new paradigm of remote collaboration through ER. It will delve into the theoretical concepts and frameworks that underpin virtual team collaboration, the technologies and tools that make ER feasible, and practical applications of ER in remote collaboration scenarios. Furthermore, it will address the challenges faced by organizations in adopting ER and offer potential solutions and future directions.

With these objectives in mind, we can now move on to designing the table of contents, ensuring that each chapter is structured to cover the necessary aspects of ER in remote collaboration. This will include introducing the core concepts, exploring the relationship between these concepts, and providing practical insights and case studies to illustrate the effectiveness and potential of ER in this domain.
