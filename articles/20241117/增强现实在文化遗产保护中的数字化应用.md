## Introduction to Augmented Reality

### Background

Augmented Reality (AR) is a technology that enhances the real-world environment by overlaying digital information, such as images, text, and sound, onto the user's view. This interactive experience merges the digital and physical worlds, providing users with enriched and immersive content. The concept of AR has been in development since the mid-20th century, but it has gained significant momentum in recent years due to advancements in computing power, mobile devices, and computer vision algorithms.

### Historical Overview

The history of AR can be traced back to the 1960s with the development of the "Virtual Reality Head-Mounted Display" by Ivan Sutherland. Over the decades, significant milestones have been achieved, such as the development of the Magic Lens in the 1990s and the introduction of mobile AR applications in the 2010s. These advancements have laid the foundation for the current state of AR technology, which is characterized by high accuracy, real-time interaction, and wide applicability.
