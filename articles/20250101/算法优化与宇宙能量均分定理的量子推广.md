The gradient descent algorithm is a fundamental optimization algorithm used to minimize a function. It works by iteratively adjusting the parameters of a model to find the minimum of the function.

### Chapter 5: Advanced Optimization Techniques

#### 5.1 Evolutionary Algorithms

Evolutionary algorithms are inspired by the process of natural selection and evolution. They use mechanisms such as mutation, crossover, and selection to evolve a population of solutions towards better ones.

#### 5.2 Particle Swarm Optimization

Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO) is a population-based optimization technique inspired by the social behavior of bird flocking or fish schooling. It updates the position and velocity of particles in a search space to find the optimal solution.

#### 5.3 Genetic Algorithms

Genetic algorithms are based on the principles of genetics and natural selection. They use operators such as selection, crossover, and mutation to evolve a population of potential solutions.
