- **时间偏差**：指投资者对时间的感知影响其决策。

def montier_model(data, alpha, beta):
    V = data['intrinsic_value']
    D = data['mood_index']
    M = data['market_participation']
    P_pred = V + alpha * D + beta * M
    return P_pred
