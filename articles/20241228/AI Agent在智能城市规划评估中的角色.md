---

#### Title: AI Agent in the Role of Smart Urban Planning and Evaluation

#### Keywords: AI Agents, Smart Urban Planning, Urban Evaluation, AI Integration

#### Abstract

The rapid urbanization of our world has brought both opportunities and challenges. To manage and optimize urban environments effectively, the integration of Artificial Intelligence (AI) into urban planning and evaluation processes has emerged as a crucial development. This book aims to provide a comprehensive exploration of AI agents in the context of smart urban planning and evaluation. By delving into the fundamental concepts, practical applications, and implementation strategies, it seeks to offer valuable insights for urban planners, policymakers, and AI practitioners.
