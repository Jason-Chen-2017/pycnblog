### 1.1 Book Overview

#### 1.1.1 AIGC and Content Moderation Introduction

**Problem Background:** 
In the age of information, the rapid proliferation of online content has brought about significant challenges. The need to moderate content has become crucial to ensure the dissemination of accurate, appropriate, and valuable information. However, traditional content moderation methods are often labor-intensive, slow, and prone to errors.

**Problem Description:** 
Content moderation involves the identification and management of inappropriate content, such as hate speech, harassment, spam, and adult content. The sheer volume of content and the complexity of language and cultural nuances make this a daunting task.

**Problem Solution:** 
The advent of Artificial Intelligence (AI) has revolutionized content moderation. AIGC, which combines AI techniques like Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs), offers a more efficient and accurate approach. It leverages the power of AI to automatically analyze and classify content.

**Boundaries and Extensions:** 
While AIGC has made significant strides, it is not a panacea. It has limitations in understanding context and cultural nuances. Therefore, it is essential to complement AI with human oversight and feedback.

**Core Concept Structure and Key Elements:** 
The core concept of AIGC content moderation revolves around the use of "思维链" (a cognitive framework). This framework integrates multiple cognitive processes, such as perception, inference, and reasoning, to understand and analyze content. The key elements include data preprocessing, model training, content classification, and post-classification analysis.
