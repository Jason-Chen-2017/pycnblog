### Mathematical Models and Formulas

To measure emotional intelligence, various models have been proposed. One such model is the Emotional Quotient Inventory (EQ-i), which uses a series of questions to evaluate an individual's emotional intelligence. Here's a LaTeX representation of the EQ-i formula:

```latex
$$
EQ_i = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n} (Q_i \times W_i)
$$

where:
EQ_i = Emotional Quotient Score
n = number of subscales
Q_i = raw score on subscale i
W_i = weight for subscale i
```
