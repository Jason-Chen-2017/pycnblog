##### 1.2 Challenges in Cross-District Team Management
- **Communication Barriers**
  - Language differences, time zone disparities, and lack of face-to-face interaction can create communication challenges.
- **Cultural Differences**
  - Different cultural backgrounds can lead to misunderstandings and conflicts within the team.
- **Time Zone Discrepancies**
  - Time zone differences can make it difficult to schedule meetings and coordinate activities among team members.
