### Introduction

In an increasingly interconnected global economy, corporate mergers and acquisitions (M&A) have become a strategic tool for companies aiming to expand their market presence and enhance competitive advantage. However, the post-acquisition integration process is often fraught with challenges that can derail even the best-laid plans. This article delves into how artificial intelligence (AI) can be harnessed to streamline and optimize the M&A integration process, ensuring that the anticipated synergies are realized and that the merged entity operates efficiently.

**Target Audience:**
This article is aimed at corporate executives, M&A advisors, strategic planners, and practitioners involved in the merger and acquisition field. It is also suitable for MBA students and researchers in related fields.
