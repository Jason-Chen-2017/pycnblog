### Basic Principles of AI Agents

**2.1 Overview of Artificial Intelligence**

AI is the simulation of human intelligence in machines that can perform tasks requiring human intelligence. Machine learning and deep learning are subfields of AI that focus on enabling computers to learn from data and improve performance over time.

**2.2 Fundamentals of Machine Learning**

Machine learning involves training algorithms to recognize patterns in data and make decisions based on those patterns. Key concepts include supervised learning, unsupervised learning, and reinforcement learning.

**2.3 Deep Learning Techniques**

Deep learning is a subset of machine learning that uses neural networks with many layers to learn from large amounts of data. Convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and recurrent neural networks (RNNs) are popular architectures in deep learning.

**2.4 Basic Architecture of AI Agents**

AI agents typically consist of sensors to perceive the environment, an internal state representation, and actuators to execute actions. Reinforcement learning is a common method for training AI agents to make decisions in uncertain environments.
