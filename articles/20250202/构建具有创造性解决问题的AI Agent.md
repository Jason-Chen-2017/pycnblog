## **Core Concepts and Principles**

### **1.1 Basic AI Concepts**

Artificial intelligence refers to the simulation of human intelligence in machines that are programmed to think like humans and mimic their actions. AI agents are a type of AI system designed to perform specific tasks autonomously, often interacting with their environment and learning from experience.

### **1.2 Creative Problem Solving**

Creative problem-solving is the process of generating innovative solutions to problems. It involves divergent thinking, which explores a wide range of ideas, and convergent thinking, which narrows down these ideas to the most viable solutions. In the context of AI, creative problem-solving aims to emulate human-like innovation through algorithmic processes.

## **Algorithm and Mathematical Models**

### **2.1 Key Algorithms**

Several algorithms are pivotal in enabling AI agents to solve problems creatively. These include genetic algorithms, neural networks, and reinforcement learning. Each of these algorithms has unique characteristics and is suited to different types of problems.

### **2.2 Mathematical Models**

Mathematical models are essential for understanding and implementing these algorithms. They provide a formal framework for describing the behavior of AI agents and the relationships between variables. Common mathematical models include probability distributions, decision trees, and graph theory.
