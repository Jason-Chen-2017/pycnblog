The rapid advancement of artificial intelligence (AI) has brought about a plethora of applications, from autonomous vehicles to virtual personal assistants. **1.1 Problem Background**

#### 1.1.1 AI Agent Definition and Importance

An AI agent is an autonomous entity that perceives its environment through sensors and acts upon it through actuators based on some decision-making model. These agents can be categorized into simple reactive agents, model-based reflex agents, goal-based agents, and learning agents. Reactive agents act based on current sensory inputs without memory of past experiences. Model-based reflex agents maintain a model of the world and use it to make decisions. Goal-based agents pursue specific goals and continuously adjust their actions to achieve these goals. Learning agents improve their behavior through experience or data.

AI agents are crucial in the development of autonomous systems because they mimic human decision-making processes and can perform tasks with minimal human intervention. From robotic arms in manufacturing to autonomous drones for surveillance, the importance of AI agents in modern technology cannot be overstated.

#### 1.1.2 Application Scenarios of AI Agents

AI agents are used in a wide range of scenarios, including:

1. **Autonomous Vehicles**: AI agents are central to the functioning of self-driving cars, which use sensors to perceive the environment and actuators to control the vehicle.
2. **Virtual Assistants**: Voice-activated virtual assistants like Siri, Alexa, and Google Assistant rely on AI agents to understand and respond to user commands.
3. **Robotic Process Automation (RPA)**: AI agents automate routine business processes by interacting with multiple systems and performing tasks without human intervention.
4. **Healthcare**: AI agents can assist in diagnosing diseases, monitoring patient health, and providing personalized medical advice.
5. **Security**: AI agents can detect and respond to security threats in real-time, such as intrusions and cyber-attacks.

#### 1.1.3 Current Challenges in AI Agent Development

Despite their importance, the development of AI agents faces several challenges:

1. **Complexity**: Designing and implementing AI agents involves understanding complex systems and processes, which can be difficult to model and simulate accurately.
2. **Scalability**: AI agents need to be scalable to handle increasing data and growing environments without significant performance degradation.
3. **Real-time Performance**: Many AI agent applications require real-time decision-making, which demands efficient algorithms and hardware acceleration.
4. **Robustness**: AI agents must be robust and reliable, capable of handling unexpected situations and errors without failing.
5. **Interoperability**: Integrating AI agents with existing systems and ensuring seamless communication between different agents can be challenging.
