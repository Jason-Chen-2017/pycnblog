### First Part: Introduction to AI Agents and Art Style Recommendation

#### Chapter 1.1: Problem Background

**Introduction of AI Agents:**
AI agents are autonomous entities designed to perform specific tasks by interacting with their environment. They operate on the principles of machine learning, deep learning, and reinforcement learning to enhance their capabilities over time.

**Art Style Recommendation:**
Art style recommendation involves identifying and suggesting styles of art that match a user's preferences or the context of a particular setting. This can range from simple stylistic categorizations to complex, context-aware suggestions.

#### Chapter 1.2: Problem Description

**Challenges in Art Style Recognition:**
- Diverse and evolving nature of art styles.
- Variability in artist techniques and interpretations.
- Lack of standardized datasets for training.

**Application Scenarios:**
- Personalized art collections for users.
- Museum digital displays.
- Art galleries and exhibitions.

#### Chapter 1.3: Solution Overview

**AI Agent Capabilities:**
AI agents can analyze vast amounts of data to recognize patterns, classify styles, and make recommendations.

**Boundary and Scope:**
The solution focuses on the use of AI agents for art style recommendation in intelligent frames, excluding other applications of AI in the art domain.
