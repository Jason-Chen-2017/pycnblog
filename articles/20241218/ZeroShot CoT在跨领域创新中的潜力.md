Zero-Shot Coreference Resolution (CoT) is a cutting-edge technique in the field of natural language processing (NLP). It aims to identify and resolve pronouns and noun phrases within a text without requiring any prior training data specific to the domain or language. Unlike traditional coreference resolution methods that rely on extensive annotated datasets, Zero-Shot CoT leverages generalizable models and transfer learning to achieve high accuracy across diverse domains.

Coreference resolution is a crucial task in NLP, enabling machines to understand the relationships between different entities within a text. However, traditional approaches often struggle when dealing with out-of-domain data or new domains that have not been encountered before. Zero-Shot CoT addresses this challenge by enabling machines to generalize and adapt to new domains with minimal or no domain-specific training data.
