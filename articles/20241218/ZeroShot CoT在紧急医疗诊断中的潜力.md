### Chapter 1: Overview of Zero-Shot Conceptualization

**1.1 Problem Background**

**1.1.1 Importance of Emergency Medical Diagnosis**
Emergency medical diagnosis plays a vital role in the prompt and accurate treatment of patients suffering from life-threatening conditions. However, traditional diagnostic methods often struggle to provide timely and precise results, leading to potential delays in treatment and compromised patient outcomes.

**1.1.2 Limitations of Traditional Medical Diagnosis**
The reliance on human expertise and the time-consuming nature of traditional diagnostic methods can result in delayed diagnosis and misdiagnosis. Moreover, the availability of diagnostic tools and resources may vary significantly across different regions, exacerbating these challenges.

**1.1.3 Introduction to Zero-Shot Conceptualization**
Zero-shot Conceptualization (CoT) is an advanced AI technique that enables machines to understand and process unknown concepts without prior training on specific examples. This capability makes it an attractive candidate for improving emergency medical diagnosis by overcoming the limitations of traditional methods.

**1.1.4 Definition and Characteristics of Zero-Shot Conceptualization**
Zero-shot CoT leverages knowledge graphs, transfer learning, and other AI techniques to infer the meaning and relationships of new concepts based on existing knowledge. This approach is characterized by its ability to generalize across domains and languages, providing a flexible and scalable solution for emergency medical diagnosis.

**1.1.5 Comparison with Traditional Methods**
Compared to traditional diagnostic methods, zero-shot CoT offers several advantages, including faster processing times, reduced dependency on human expertise, and the ability to handle diverse medical concepts and languages.

**1.1.6 Potential of Zero-Shot Conceptualization in Emergency Medical Diagnosis**
The potential of zero-shot CoT in emergency medical diagnosis lies in its ability to provide rapid and accurate diagnostic support, enabling healthcare professionals to make informed decisions and improve patient outcomes. This chapter concludes with a discussion on the current application status and future prospects of zero-shot CoT in emergency medical diagnosis.
