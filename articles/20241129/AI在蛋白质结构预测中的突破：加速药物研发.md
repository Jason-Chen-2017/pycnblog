#### 1.2 The Importance of Protein Structure Prediction

Proteins are the workhorses of the cell, and their three-dimensional structures dictate their functions. Accurately predicting protein structures is crucial for several reasons:

- **Targeted Drug Design**: Understanding protein structures helps in identifying potential drug targets and designing molecules that can interact with these targets.
- **Biological Research**: Protein structure prediction facilitates the study of protein families, evolution, and functional relationships.
- **Medical Diagnostics**: Predicted protein structures can aid in the development of diagnostic tools for diseases caused by protein misfolding.
