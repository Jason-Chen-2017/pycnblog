To ensure clarity and precision, we need to define key technical terms and concepts used in the book. This will help readers who may not be familiar with specific jargon or concepts related to Zero-Shot CoT.

#### Key Terms and Concepts

1. **Zero-Shot Core-Text (CoT)**: A concept in natural language processing where a model can generate coherent and contextually appropriate responses without being trained on specific categories or domains.

2. **Word Embeddings**: A representation of words as dense vectors in a multi-dimensional space, which capture semantic relationships between words.

3. **Neural Networks**: A series of algorithms that attempt to recognize underlying relationships in a set of data through a process that mimics the way the human brain operates.

4. **Transfer Learning**: The technique of using a pre-trained model on a different but related task to improve the model's performance on the target task.

5. **Multi-Modal Learning**: The process of training models to understand and process information from multiple input sources, such as text, images, and audio.

6. **Natural Language Inference (NLI)**: A task in artificial intelligence where a model must determine if the meaning of one sentence (the hypothesis) is implied by another sentence (the premise).

7. **Latent Dirichlet Allocation (LDA)**: A generative statistical model that allows sets of observations to be explained by unobserved groups that explain why such observations occur.
