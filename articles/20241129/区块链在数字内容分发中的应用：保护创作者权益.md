### Abstract
This book provides a comprehensive exploration of the integration of blockchain technology in digital content distribution, with a focus on protecting the rights of content creators. It begins with an overview of blockchain technology, explaining its core concepts and principles. The book then dives into the application of blockchain in digital content distribution, discussing smart contracts, tokenization, and blockchain-based content distribution platforms. It also explores how blockchain can be used to protect intellectual property, manage digital rights, and ensure provenance and traceability. Through detailed case studies and practical applications, the book illustrates the potential and limitations of blockchain in this domain. Finally, it examines the future trends and challenges in the adoption of blockchain technology in digital content distribution, offering insights and recommendations for the industry.
