# 定义优化目标函数和约束条件

result = linprog(c, A_ub=A, b_ub=b, bounds=[(0, None), (0, None)])

$$
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
    \text{最大化} \quad & Z = c^T x \\

\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
