The first step in creating a detailed and concise table of contents is to understand the book's main focus. "New Zen Lifestyle: The Application of Eastern Wisdom in Modern Life" aims to explore how ancient Eastern philosophies, particularly Zen Buddhism, can be applied to modern life. This involves understanding key aspects of Zen, how they relate to daily living, and how they can improve overall well-being.

### Step 2: Identify Core Sections and Subsections

Based on the book's title and intended audience, we can identify several core sections and their corresponding subsections. These sections should cover various aspects of Zen philosophy and its practical application in today's world. Some potential sections and subsections could include:

- **Introduction to Zen and Eastern Wisdom**
  - Historical background of Zen
  - Key figures in Zen Buddhism
  - Core teachings and principles
  - Comparison with other Eastern philosophies

- **Zen Philosophy in Daily Life**
  - Applying Zen principles in daily routines
  - Simplifying life with Zen
  - Mindfulness in daily activities
  - The role of simplicity in Zen

- **Mindfulness and Meditation**
  - Basics of mindfulness
  - Techniques for meditation
  - Benefits of mindfulness and meditation
  - Integrating mindfulness into daily life

- **Zen in Relationships**
  - The essence of Zen in human interactions
  - Developing compassionate relationships
  - The importance of non-attachment in relationships

- **Zen in Work and Productivity**
  - Zen principles for effective work
  - Managing stress and time in the workplace
  - Balancing work and personal life

- **Zen in Health and Well-being**
  - The connection between mind and body
  - Practices for maintaining physical and mental health
  - The role of Zen in disease prevention

- **Zen in Creativity and Arts**
  - Zen principles in artistic expression
  - The role of creativity in Zen practice
  - Examples of Zen in different art forms

- **Conclusion and Practical Applications**
  - Summarizing the key takeaways
  - Practical advice for incorporating Zen into daily life
  - Challenges and solutions

### Step 3: Organize Content in a Logical Structure

To ensure the book provides a comprehensive and engaging read, it's important to organize the content in a logical structure. This means starting with an introduction to Zen and its history, followed by a detailed exploration of Zen principles and their application in various aspects of life. The final sections can focus on practical tips and a conclusion that summarizes the main ideas.
