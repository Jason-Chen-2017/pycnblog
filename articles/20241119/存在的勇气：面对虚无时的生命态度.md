### Step 4: Understanding Nihilism and Its Impact

**4.1 Definition and Origins of Nihilism**

- **Historical Perspectives**:
  Nihilism has its roots in various philosophical movements, including early Christianity, Renaissance humanism, and Enlightenment rationalism. However, it gained prominence in the 19th and 20th centuries, particularly with figures like Friedrich Nietzsche and Jean-Paul Sartre.

- **Key Theorists and Their Ideas**:
  - **Friedrich Nietzsche**:
    Nietzsche posited that the death of God marked the onset of nihilism, leading individuals to face the absence of objective moral values and the meaning of life. He advocated for the overcoming of nihilism through the affirmation of one's own will to power.
    
  - **Jean-Paul Sartre**:
    Sartre's existentialism emphasized the idea that existence precedes essence, asserting that individuals are free to create their own values and meanings in the absence of inherent purpose.
