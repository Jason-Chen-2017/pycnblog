### 1.1.1 Background of Zero-Shot Learning

Zero-shot learning (ZSL) is a branch of machine learning that allows models to classify or predict outcomes for unseen classes without any prior training data for those classes. It is particularly useful in real-world scenarios where the model must generalize to new, previously unseen data without extensive retraining. The development of ZSL has been driven by the increasing complexity of datasets and the need for robust, adaptable AI systems.

```markdown
### 1.1.2 The Concept of CoT (Conceptual Blending Theory)

Conceptual Blending Theory (CoT) posits that human-like reasoning involves combining multiple concepts to generate new inferences. This theory has inspired several AI models, including those in ZSL, which attempt to mimic this process. CoT is rooted in cognitive psychology and offers a framework for understanding how AI can achieve zero-shot learning by leveraging abstract concept representations.
```
