## Theoretical Foundations of Self-Consistency CoT

### Core Concepts and Relationships
Self-Consistency CoT is built on several core concepts, including consistency, reliability, and trust. Consistency refers to the degree of agreement between the outputs of an AI system under different conditions or with different inputs. Reliability, on the other hand, measures the accuracy and dependability of the AI-generated answers. Trust is the confidence that users place in the AI system's outputs.

### Mathematical Models and Formulas
To quantify the consistency of AI answers, we need mathematical models and formulas. One such model is the Consistency Score (CS), which is calculated as follows:

$$
CS = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n} |A_i - B_i|}{n}
$$

where \( A_i \) and \( B_i \) represent the outputs of the AI system under different conditions or with different inputs, and \( n \) is the total number of conditions or inputs.

### Algorithm Principles and Pseudo-Code
The algorithm for calculating the Consistency Score is relatively simple. The core principle is to measure the absolute difference between the outputs under different conditions or inputs, and then average these differences to obtain the Consistency Score.

Pseudo-code for the Consistency Score calculation:

```
function calculateConsistencyScore(outputs):
    differences = []
    for i in range(len(outputs)):
        for j in range(i+1, len(outputs)):
            difference = abs(outputs[i] - outputs[j])
            differences.append(difference)
    consistencyScore = sum(differences) / len(differences)
    return consistencyScore
```
