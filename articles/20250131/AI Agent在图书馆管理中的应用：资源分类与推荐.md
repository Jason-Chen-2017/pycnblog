### Introduction

In recent years, the integration of artificial intelligence (AI) into various sectors has led to significant advancements in efficiency and accuracy. Among these sectors, library management stands out due to its inherent complexity and the vast amount of data involved. The role of AI agents, specifically in the context of resource classification and recommendation, has emerged as a transformative innovation. This article aims to explore the potential of AI agents in library management, focusing on how they can revolutionize the way resources are categorized and recommended to users.

### Abstract

The primary objective of this article is to delve into the application of AI agents in library management, with a particular emphasis on resource classification and recommendation systems. ### Background

#### 1.1 Problem Background

The traditional library management system faces numerous challenges, including inefficiencies in resource organization, limited accessibility, and the difficulty of providing personalized user experiences. The rapid growth of digital libraries and the increasing diversity of resources have further compounded these issues. AI agents offer a promising solution by leveraging advanced machine learning algorithms and natural language processing techniques to automate tasks and enhance user interactions.

#### 1.2 Definition and Potential

AI agents are software entities designed to perform tasks autonomously based on input data and predefined objectives. In the context of library management, AI agents can be utilized for resource classification, which involves organizing resources into hierarchical categories, and for recommendation systems, which suggest resources to users based on their preferences and behaviors.

### Core Concepts and Relationships

#### 1.4 AI Agents

AI agents are software programs that mimic human-like behavior. They are equipped with algorithms that allow them to learn from data, make decisions, and perform tasks autonomously. In the context of library management, AI agents can be trained to understand the content and structure of resources, categorize them accurately, and suggest relevant resources to users.

#### 1.5 Resource Classification and Recommendation Systems

Resource classification involves organizing resources into logical categories, making it easier for users to find and access the information they need. Recommendation systems, on the other hand, analyze user preferences and behavior to suggest resources that are likely to be of interest to them. The two systems are closely related, as accurate classification is crucial for effective recommendations.

#### 1.6 Core Concept Connections

The integration of AI agents with resource classification and recommendation systems creates a synergistic effect, leveraging the strengths of each component. AI agents can learn from user interactions, improving classification accuracy over time, and enhancing the quality of recommendations.
