#### **4.2 Observer Pattern**

* **Basic Principles:**
The observer pattern allows objects to be notified of changes in state by other objects.

* **Applications:**
It is widely used in event-driven programming and real-time systems.

* **Advantages and Disadvantages:**
The pattern is flexible but can lead to complex dependency management.

#### **4.3 Publish-Subscribe Pattern**

* **Basic Principles:**
In this pattern, components subscribe to specific events or messages, and the system notifies them when those events occur.

* **Applications:**
It is often used in event-driven architectures to decouple senders and receivers.

* **Advantages and Disadvantages:**
It allows for a high degree of decoupling but can be more complex to implement.
