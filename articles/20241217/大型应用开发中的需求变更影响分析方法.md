- **User Requirements Evolving Over Time**: As users interact with the application, they may discover new needs or realize that existing features are inadequate. For example, a social media platform may introduce a new feature based on user feedback, such as live streaming or group chat.
- **Technological Advancements and Market Trends**: Rapid advancements in technology and changes in market trends can necessitate updates to the application to remain competitive. - **Business Strategy Adjustments**: Shifts in business strategies may require the application to support new functionalities or integrate with different systems. #### 1.2.2 Types of Demand Changes

Demand changes can be categorized into several types:

- **Requirements Changes**: These involve modifications to the functional or non-functional requirements of the application. For example, a change in the user interface design or the addition of new features.
- **Scope Changes**: Changes in the project scope can involve adding or removing features, which can significantly impact project timelines and resources. Expanding the scope of an application to include additional functionalities may require additional time and resources.
- **Schedule Changes**: Adjustments to the project schedule may be necessary to accommodate demand changes or address unforeseen delays. For example, if a critical feature is delayed, the project timeline may need to be adjusted to ensure that the overall project remains on track.
- **Budget Changes**: Changes in the project budget can arise from cost overruns or the need to allocate resources to address demand changes.
