### Abstract
The advent of wearable devices has revolutionized the field of health monitoring, enabling continuous and real-time tracking of vital signs and health metrics. This paper delves into the realm of time series analysis as a mathematical tool to extract meaningful insights from the vast amounts of data generated by wearable devices. We will explore the fundamentals of time series analysis, the mathematical tools and algorithms used, and their practical applications in health monitoring. By understanding these concepts and methods, we aim to provide a comprehensive guide for professionals and researchers in the field of wearable technology and health analytics.
