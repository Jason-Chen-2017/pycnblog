$$ P(A \cap B) = P(A) \cdot P(B|A) $$

其中，$P(A)$ 是事件 $A$ 的概率，$P(B|A)$ 是在事件 $A$ 发生的条件下事件 $B$ 的概率。

$$ P = \begin{bmatrix} 0.6 & 0.4 \\ 0.2 & 0.8 \end{bmatrix} $$

在这个例子中，状态转移矩阵 $P$ 表示从状态 1 转换到状态 2 的概率为 0.6，从状态 2 转换到状态 1 的概率为 0.4。同样，从状态 1 转换到状态 1 的概率为 0.2，从状态 2 转换到状态 2 的概率为 0.8。

##### 4.3.2 熵与信息增益的案例分析

假设一个系统有两个状态：状态 1 和状态 2。以下是一个熵与信息增益的例子：

$$ H(X) = -\sum_{x \in X} P(x) \cdot \log_2 P(x) $$

$$ P(X = 1) = 0.6, P(X = 2) = 0.4 $$

$$ H(X) = -0.6 \cdot \log_2 0.6 - 0.4 \cdot \log_2 0.4 \approx 0.9219 $$

$$ I(X; Y) = H(X) - H(X | Y) $$

$$ P(Y = 1 | X = 1) = 0.8, P(Y = 2 | X = 1) = 0.2 $$

$$ P(Y = 1 | X = 2) = 0.3, P(Y = 2 | X = 2) = 0.7 $$

$$ H(X | Y) = -\sum_{x \in X} P(x | y) \cdot \log_2 P(x | y) $$

$$ H(X | Y) = -0.8 \cdot \log_2 0.8 - 0.2 \cdot \log_2 0.2 - 0.3 \cdot \log_2 0.3 - 0.7 \cdot \log_2 0.7 \approx 0.4686 $$

```python
import numpy as np
import tensorflow as tf

# 参数设置
learning_rate = 0.01
num_iterations = 1000

# 意识涌现模型
class ConsciousnessEmergenceModel(tf.keras.Model):
    def __init__(self):
        super(ConsciousnessEmergenceModel, self).__init__()
        self.dense1 = tf.keras.layers.Dense(64, activation='relu')
        self.dense2 = tf.keras.layers.Dense(1, activation='sigmoid')

    @tf.function
    def call(self, inputs, training=False):
        x = self.dense1(inputs)
        outputs = self.dense2(x)
        return outputs

# 意识涌现算法实现
def train_model(model, train_data, test_data, learning_rate, num_iterations):
    optimizer = tf.keras.optimizers.Adam(learning_rate=learning_rate)
    loss_fn = tf.keras.losses.BinaryCrossentropy()

    for i in range(num_iterations):
        with tf.GradientTape() as tape:
            predictions = model(train_data, training=True)
            loss = loss_fn(train_data, predictions)

        gradients = tape.gradient(loss, model.trainable_variables)
        optimizer.apply_gradients(zip(gradients, model.trainable_variables))

        if i % 100 == 0:
            print(f"Epoch {i}, Loss: {loss.numpy()}")

    # 测试模型
    test_predictions = model(test_data, training=False)
    test_loss = loss_fn(test_data, test_predictions)
    print(f"Test Loss: {test_loss.numpy()}")

# 实现意识涌现算法
model = ConsciousnessEmergenceModel()
train_model(model, train_data, test_data, learning_rate, num_iterations)
```
