As the field progressed, so did the methodologies for problem decomposition and algorithm design. Researchers began to develop more sophisticated algorithms that could handle larger and more complex problems. Techniques such as dynamic programming, graph algorithms, and heuristic methods were introduced to improve the efficiency and scalability of algorithm design. However, even with these advancements, traditional algorithms often faced limitations when dealing with multi-step problems that required iterative processing and coordination.
