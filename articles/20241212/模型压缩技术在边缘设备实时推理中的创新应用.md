## Step 3: Algorithm Theory and Explanation

### **3.1 Quantization**

**Theory and Mathematical Model:**
Quantization reduces the precision of the model's weights and biases, resulting in a smaller model size. The formula for quantizing weights is:
$$
x_{quant} = \text{round}(x / \beta) \times \beta
$$

**Algorithm Flowchart:**
```mermaid

```
