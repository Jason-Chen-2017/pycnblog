**1.1 Problem Background**

Forest fires have become an increasingly severe environmental and economic problem worldwide. The frequency and severity of these fires are rising due to various factors, including climate change, deforestation, and human activities. The consequences are devastating, leading to loss of life, property damage, and significant ecological degradation.

**1.2 Problem Description**

The problem of forest fires is multifaceted. It involves monitoring vast areas of land for potential fire hazards, analyzing real-time data from various sources, and making timely decisions to prevent or mitigate the spread of fires. Traditional methods have limitations in terms of coverage, accuracy, and responsiveness.

**1.3 Problem Solution**

Enter prompt-driven intelligent forest fire warning systems. These systems utilize advanced algorithms, machine learning models, and real-time data processing capabilities to predict and warn of impending forest fires with high accuracy and efficiency. By doing so, they can significantly improve disaster prevention and response times.
