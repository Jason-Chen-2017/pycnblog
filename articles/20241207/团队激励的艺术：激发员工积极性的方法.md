### Step 3: Discuss Team Motivation Theories
- **Introduction to Motivation Theory**: Briefly discuss the history and evolution of motivation theories, from early behavioral theories to contemporary cognitive and emotional theories.
- **Key Motivation Theories**: 
  - **H马斯洛的需求层次理论（Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs）**:
    - **Physiological Needs**: Food, water, shelter.
    - **Safety Needs**: Security, health, employment.
    - **Social Needs**: Belongingness, friendship.
    - **Esteem Needs**: Self-esteem, confidence.
    - **Self-Actualization**: Personal growth, fulfilling potential.
  - **赫茨伯格的双因素理论（Herzberg's Two-Factor Theory）**:
    - **Hygiene Factors**: Working conditions, salary.
    - **Motivators**: Achievement, recognition, responsibility.
  - **维克托·弗鲁姆的期望理论（Victor Vroom's Expectancy Theory）**:
    - **Valence**: The value an individual places on a desired outcome.
    - **Expectancy**: The belief that effort will lead to performance.
    - **Instrumentality**: The belief that performance will lead to the desired outcome.

- **Organizational Factors**:

  - **Economic Conditions**: The state of the economy and its impact on job security.
