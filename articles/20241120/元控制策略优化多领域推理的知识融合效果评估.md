**Common Evaluation Metrics**

Several evaluation metrics can be used to assess the effectiveness of knowledge fusion in multi-domain reasoning. These metrics include:

1. **Accuracy**: This metric measures the percentage of correct predictions or classifications made by the system.
2. **Precision and Recall**: Precision measures the proportion of positive identifications that are correct, while recall measures the proportion of actual positives that are identified correctly.
3. **F1 Score**: The F1 score is the harmonic mean of precision and recall, providing a balanced measure of the system's performance.
4. **Confusion Matrix**: This matrix summarizes the performance of the system by showing the number of correct and incorrect predictions for each class.
5. **Mean Absolute Error (MAE)** and **Mean Squared Error (MSE)**: These metrics measure the average error between the predicted and actual values, with MSE being more sensitive to large errors.
