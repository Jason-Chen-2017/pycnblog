The advent of advanced imaging techniques, such as CT and MRI, has enabled the precise identification and localization of tumors. This is where artificial intelligence (AI) comes into play. AI has the potential to revolutionize radiation therapy by automating and optimizing the planning process, from tumor localization to dose optimization. By leveraging machine learning algorithms, AI can analyze vast amounts of medical data to identify patterns and make predictions that can inform treatment decisions. This not only improves the accuracy and efficiency of radiation therapy but also enhances patient outcomes.
