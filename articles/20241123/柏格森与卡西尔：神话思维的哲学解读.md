The book "Bergson and Cassirer: A Philosophical Reading of Mythical Thought" will primarily focus on two key philosophers: Henri Bergson and Ernst Cassirer. Bergson's philosophy revolves around the concept of élan vital, which emphasizes the vitality and creativity inherent in living organisms. Cassirer, on the other hand, is known for his cultural philosophy and his theory of symbolic forms, which suggests that human thought and culture are shaped by symbols. The key themes of the book will be the philosophical interpretations of mythical thought through the works of these two thinkers.
