#### Introduction

##### The Significance of Health Monitoring

Health monitoring has become an integral part of modern healthcare. It helps in the early detection of health issues, improves the quality of care, and reduces the overall cost of treatment. With the advent of wearable devices, mobile apps, and IoT technologies, it has become easier to collect and analyze health data.

##### The Importance of Early Warning Systems

Early warning systems are designed to detect and predict health issues before they become severe. They provide timely alerts, enabling healthcare providers to intervene early and prevent complications. These systems can save lives and significantly improve patient outcomes.

##### The Role of Trigger Words

Trigger words are key indicators that suggest the presence of a health issue. They can be detected through various data sources, such as medical records, wearable device readings, and even social media posts. By identifying these words, early warning systems can initiate timely interventions.
