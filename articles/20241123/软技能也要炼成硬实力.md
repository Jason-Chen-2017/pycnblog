                 



### Step 1: Defining the Core Concepts

To begin with, we need to define the core concepts that will form the foundation of our article, "软技能也要炼成硬实力." Soft skills are those personal attributes that enable individuals to effectively communicate and interact with others. They include qualities such as emotional intelligence, teamwork, adaptability, and problem-solving. In contrast, hard skills are the specific technical abilities that are directly related to a job, such as coding, database management, or project management.

The transformation from soft to hard skills involves the development and enhancement of soft skills to make them more tangible and applicable in professional settings. This process is crucial in today's workplace, where the ability to work effectively in teams and adapt to new technologies is just as important as having technical expertise.
