  $$ FCFF = EBIT(1 - 税率) + 折旧 - 资本支出 $$  

  - 折现率计算  
  $$ r = WACC = (D/E) \times (Cost\ of\ debt) \times (1 - 税率) + (E/V) \times Cost\ of\ equity $$
