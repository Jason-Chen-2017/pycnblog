### 1.3 创新能力评估的核心要素组成

### 3.2 算法实现代码
```python
def calculate_innovation_score(criteria_scores, weights):
    return sum(criteria_scores[i] * weights[i] for i in range(len(criteria_scores)))

criteria_scores = [0.8, 0.9, 0.7]  # 示例指标得分
weights = [0.4, 0.3, 0.3]  # 示例权重
score = calculate_innovation_score(criteria_scores, weights)
print(f"创新能力得分为: {score}")
```

### 3.3 算法数学模型
$$ \text{创新能力得分} = \sum_{i=1}^{n} w_i \times s_i $$

其中：
- \( w_i \) 是第 \( i \) 个指标的权重
- \( s_i \) 是第 \( i \) 个指标的得分
