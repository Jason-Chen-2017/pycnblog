#### Key Concepts and Terms

To begin, let's define some fundamental concepts and terms that will be pivotal in our discussion:

- **AIGC (AI-Generated Content):** AIGC refers to the generation of content, such as text, images, or videos, using artificial intelligence, specifically large language models like GPT-3 or DALL-E. This technology leverages vast datasets to create highly coherent and contextually relevant content.

- **Food Safety Early Warning System:** A system designed to monitor and predict potential hazards in the food supply chain, ensuring the safety of consumers. This system typically involves real-time data collection, analysis, and alerts for timely intervention.

- **Future Food Safety:** The concept of food safety evolving with technological advancements, particularly in the realm of AI and machine learning, to address emerging challenges and enhance the overall safety of the food supply chain.
