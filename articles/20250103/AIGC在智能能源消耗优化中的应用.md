**Operation Management**: Power systems require constant monitoring and control to ensure stable and reliable power supply. This involves managing generation, transmission, and distribution processes to meet demand and maintain system stability.

### 2.2 AIGC Applications in Power Systems

**Load Forecasting**: AIGC can accurately predict power demand, enabling better planning and resource allocation.

- **Efficiency Improvement**: The optimized load dispatching reduced transmission losses by 15%, improving overall system efficiency.
- **Maintenance Cost Reduction**: Proactive maintenance based on fault predictions reduced maintenance costs by 25%.

These results demonstrate the significant potential of AIGC in power system optimization, highlighting the benefits of leveraging advanced AI technologies to enhance energy management and reliability.
