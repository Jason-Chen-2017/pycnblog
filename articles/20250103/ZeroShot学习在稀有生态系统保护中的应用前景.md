### Step 1: Introduction to Zero-Shot Learning

Zero-Shot Learning (ZSL) is a concept in machine learning that allows a model to classify or predict the properties of unseen classes without any prior exposure to these classes during training. This is particularly useful in scenarios where data for the target classes are scarce or non-existent. ZSL has gained significant attention in various fields due to its ability to leverage prior knowledge and generalize to new, unseen scenarios.
