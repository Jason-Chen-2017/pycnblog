#### Mathematical Models and Formulas

**4.1 Introduction to Mathematical Models**

Mathematical models are used to represent real-world phenomena and systems in a mathematical form. In the context of ECI, mathematical models are used to describe traffic patterns, predict traffic conditions, and optimize routing.

**4.2 Explanation of Key Formulas**

Some of the key mathematical formulas used in ECI include:

- **Traffic Flow Model**: This model describes the relationship between traffic volume, density, and speed. The fundamental traffic flow model is given by the formula:

  $$q = k \cdot \frac{u^3}{(1 - \frac{u}{v})^{2}}$$

  where \( q \) is the traffic flow, \( k \) is a constant, \( u \) is the traffic density, and \( v \) is the traffic speed.

- **Traffic Prediction Model**: This model is used to predict traffic conditions based on historical data and current conditions. A common traffic prediction model is the Autoregressive Integrated Moving Average (ARIMA) model, given by the formula:

  $$X_t = \varphi_0 + \varphi_1 X_{t-1} + \varphi_2 X_{t-2} + ... + \varphi_p X_{t-p} + \theta_1 \epsilon_{t-1} + \theta_2 \epsilon_{t-2} + ... + \theta_q \epsilon_{t-q} + \epsilon_t$$

  where \( X_t \) is the traffic value at time \( t \), \( \varphi \) and \( \theta \) are parameters, and \( \epsilon \) is the error term.

**4.3 Example Applications**

**Example 1: Traffic Flow Prediction**

Suppose we have traffic flow data for a particular road segment over the past 24 hours. We can use the ARIMA model to predict the traffic flow for the next hour.

```python
import pandas as pd
from statsmodels.tsa.arima.model import ARIMA

# Load traffic flow data
data = pd.read_csv('traffic_data.csv')
data['date'] = pd.to_datetime(data['date'])
data.set_index('date', inplace=True)

# Fit ARIMA model
model = ARIMA(data['traffic_flow'], order=(1, 1, 1))
model_fit = model.fit()

# Make prediction
forecast = model_fit.forecast(steps=1)
print(forecast)
```

```python

# Define traffic flow model parameters
```
