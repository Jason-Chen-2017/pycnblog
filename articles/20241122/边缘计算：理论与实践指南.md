                 



### Introduction to the Book and Main Topics

**Title: Edge Computing: Theory and Practice Guide**

**Keywords: Edge Computing, IoT, Distributed Systems, AI, Real-Time Analytics**

**Abstract:**
"Edge Computing: Theory and Practice Guide" is a comprehensive resource designed to introduce the fundamental concepts, architectures, algorithms, mathematical models, and practical applications of edge computing. The book aims to serve as a guide for both beginners and advanced practitioners in the field of computer science and IoT. By delving into the theoretical underpinnings and practical implementations, the book provides readers with a deep understanding of how edge computing can revolutionize data processing, efficiency, and real-time decision-making.

This book is organized into several major sections, each building upon the previous to create a cohesive and informative journey through the world of edge computing. The initial chapters cover foundational topics, while later sections dive into advanced concepts and practical applications. Throughout, the book emphasizes the importance of edge computing in the context of modern technology and its potential to address the limitations of traditional centralized computing models.
