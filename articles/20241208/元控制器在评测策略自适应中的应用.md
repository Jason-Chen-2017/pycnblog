### What is a meta-controller?
A meta-controller is a control loop that manages other control loops. It's used to optimize the performance of a system by adjusting the parameters of its sub-control loops based on real-time feedback.

### What is adaptive evaluation strategy?
Adaptive evaluation strategy refers to the ability of an evaluation system to adjust its evaluation criteria or methods dynamically based on the context or the performance of the entities being evaluated.
