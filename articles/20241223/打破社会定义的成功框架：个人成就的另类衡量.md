                 



### Step 1: Introduction and Background

**Chapter 1: Introduction to the Concept of Alternative Success Metrics**

**1.1 Background and Definition of Socially Defined Success**
In our modern society, success is often narrowly defined by conventional metrics such as wealth, career achievements, and social status. This societal definition can be traced back to historical and cultural norms that prioritize material wealth and societal recognition as the ultimate goals of life. However, this traditional view of success is not universally applicable or satisfying for everyone.

**1.2 Rationale for Alternative Success Metrics**
The need for alternative success metrics arises from the recognition that individuals have diverse aspirations and values. Some may prioritize personal fulfillment, happiness, or community impact over traditional markers of success. By considering alternative metrics, we encourage a more holistic and inclusive approach to defining success, one that accommodates a wider range of personal goals and life experiences.

**1.3 Personal and Societal Implications**
Adopting alternative success metrics can have profound personal and societal implications. On an individual level, it can lead to greater personal satisfaction and a sense of purpose. On a societal level, it can foster a more equitable and diverse culture, where individuals are not solely judged by their social or economic status.

### Step 3: Theories and Frameworks

**Chapter 3: Theoretical Foundations of Alternative Success Metrics**

**3.1 Psychological Theories on Success**

**3.1.1 Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs**
Abraham Maslow's theory posits that individuals have a hierarchical set of needs that must be met in a specific order, from basic physiological needs to self-actualization.

$$
\text{Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs} = \begin{cases}
\text{Physiological} & \text{if } N_1 \leq N_2 \leq \ldots \leq N_5 \\
\text{Self-Actualization} & \text{if all needs are met}
\end{cases}
$$

**3.1.2 Self-Determination Theory**
Self-Determination Theory by Deci and Ryan emphasizes the importance of autonomy, competence, and relatedness in achieving personal satisfaction and success.

**3.1.3 Mermaid Flowchart of Psychological Theories**
