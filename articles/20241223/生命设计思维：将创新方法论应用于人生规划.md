### I. Introduction to Life Design Thinking

#### 1.1 The Concept of Life Design Thinking

**1.1.1 Origins and Evolution of Life Design Thinking**

- **Background Introduction:**
  - Life design thinking emerged as a response to the complexities of modern life, drawing from various disciplines such as design thinking, psychology, and systems thinking.
  - It has evolved over time, with roots in the fields of human-centered design and positive psychology.

- **Problem Description:**
  - People often struggle with creating a meaningful and purposeful life due to the rapid pace of change and the overwhelming number of choices.

- **Solution and Boundaries:**
  - Life design thinking provides a framework to navigate these challenges by focusing on personal vision, goal setting, and iterative experimentation.
  - The boundaries of life design thinking include personal well-being, career planning, and lifestyle choices.

- **Core Concepts and Components:**
  - **Life Vision:** A clear and inspiring image of the future you want to create.
  - **Purpose:** The underlying reason for pursuing your life vision.
  - **Goals:** Specific, measurable, and time-bound milestones that contribute to your life vision.

**1.1.2 Key Principles and Elements of Life Design Thinking**

- **Core Principles:**
  - **Human-Centered:** Focus on understanding and fulfilling personal needs and desires.
  - **Iterative:** Embrace experimentation and continuous improvement.
  - **Emotional Intelligence:** Recognize and manage emotions in decision-making.

- **Key Elements:**
  - **Self-Reflection:** Regularly assess your thoughts, feelings, and actions.
  - **Empathy:** Understand and consider the perspectives of others.
  - **Creativity:** Generate new ideas and approaches to solve problems.

**1.1.3 Distinctions from Traditional Life Planning**

- **Comparative Analysis:**
  - Traditional life planning tends to be more linear and rigid, focusing on specific outcomes.
  - Life design thinking, on the other hand, is more flexible and adaptable, emphasizing personal growth and well-being.

- **Advantages:**
  - Life design thinking promotes a holistic approach to life planning, considering emotional, social, and environmental aspects.
  - It empowers individuals to take an active role in shaping their future.

### II. Foundations of Life Design

- **Solution:**

**2.1.2 Defining Your Life Vision and Goals**

- **Concept Explanation:**
  - A life vision is a long-term, aspirational picture of your desired future.
  - Goals are the specific steps and milestones that will help you achieve your life vision.

  - Without a clear vision and goals, individuals may feel directionless or stuck in their lives.

- **Solution:**

- **Solution:**
  - Strategies such as creating daily routines, setting boundaries, and regularly evaluating your progress can help align your life with your purpose.
