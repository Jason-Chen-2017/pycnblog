#### 第1章: 问题背景与核心概念

##### 1.1 问题背景

The world's ancient scripts, such as Egyptian hieroglyphs, Cuneiform, and Mayan hieroglyphs, have always captivated scholars and historians. These scripts provide invaluable insights into ancient civilizations and their languages. However, deciphering ancient scripts is an arduous task due to the complexity and diversity of these writing systems. With the advent of artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning, there is a new hope for advancing the field of ancient script decipherment.

##### 1.2 Core Concepts

###### 1.2.1 AIGC

Artificial Intelligence Generated Content (AIGC) refers to the process of creating content, such as text, images, and audio, using artificial intelligence. AIGC leverages deep learning techniques, including Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) and Variational Autoencoders (VAEs), to generate content that is similar to real-world data.

###### 1.2.2 Ancient Script Decipherment

Ancient script decipherment involves the recognition, translation, and interpretation of ancient writing systems. This task is challenging due to the significant differences between ancient and modern writing systems and the loss of many ancient scripts.

###### 1.2.3 Cross-Era Language Understanding

Cross-era language understanding is the ability to comprehend and communicate between languages from different historical periods. This is crucial for ancient script decipherment as it allows for the understanding of languages from various eras.
