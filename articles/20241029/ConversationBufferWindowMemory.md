        if buffer is not full:
            append word to buffer
        else:
            remove the oldest word from buffer and append the new word

对话缓冲窗口内存模型的数学模型可以表示为：

$$
M = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} \mathbb{E}[p(x|C) - p(x)]
