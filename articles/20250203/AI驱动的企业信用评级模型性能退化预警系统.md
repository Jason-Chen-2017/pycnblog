                 



### Step 1: Introduction to the Book and Background
#### Chapter 1: Problem Background and System Overview

**1.1 Problem Background**

In today's financial market, the importance of credit rating cannot be overstated. Credit rating agencies evaluate the creditworthiness of companies, providing ratings that influence investment decisions, loan approvals, and corporate financing. However, the traditional credit rating models face several challenges, including data scarcity, non-stationarity of data, and model overfitting. These challenges have led to performance degradation and potential risks in credit rating systems.

**1.2 Credit Rating Model Overview**

Credit rating models typically consist of three main components: data collection and preprocessing, feature engineering, and model selection and training. These models aim to predict the credit risk of a company based on historical financial data, industry trends, and market conditions.

**1.3 AI in Credit Rating Models**

Artificial Intelligence (AI), particularly machine learning, has revolutionized the field of credit rating. AI-driven models can handle large volumes of data, uncover hidden patterns, and adapt to changing market conditions. However, the adoption of AI also brings new challenges, such as algorithmic bias and interpretability issues.

**1.4 Performance Degradation Warning System Overview**

Performance degradation warning systems are designed to monitor and detect any decline in the performance of credit rating models. These systems can provide early warnings, allowing stakeholders to take corrective actions before potential financial losses occur.

**1.5 Summary**

This chapter provides a comprehensive overview of the challenges faced by traditional credit rating models and the potential of AI-driven models to address these issues. It also introduces the concept of performance degradation warning systems and their importance in ensuring the accuracy and reliability of credit ratings.
