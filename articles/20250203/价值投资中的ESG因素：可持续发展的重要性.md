## Introduction to Value Investment and ESG Factors

### Key Terms

- **Value Investment**: An investment strategy that seeks to identify undervalued securities in the market, aiming to purchase them at a price below their intrinsic value, with the expectation that their market value will eventually rise.
- **ESG Factors**: Environmental, Social, and Governance factors that investors consider when evaluating the sustainability and ethical impact of a company. These factors help investors make more informed decisions and align their portfolios with their values.
- **Sustainability**: The ability of an organization to meet its current needs without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs.

### Abstract

This article delves into the world of value investment, focusing on the integration of ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance) factors. We'll explore the historical background and evolution of sustainable investing, the core concepts and principles of ESG, and how these factors impact financial performance. Through practical applications and industry-specific analyses, we'll guide investors in incorporating ESG into their value investment strategies. The article will also discuss the regulatory landscape and future trends, concluding with best practices for sustainable investing and a forward-looking perspective on the future of value investment and sustainability.
