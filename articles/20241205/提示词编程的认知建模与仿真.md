### Introduction to the Book

**Title:** Cognitive Modeling and Simulation of Prompt-Based Programming

**Keywords:** Cognitive Modeling, Prompt-Based Programming, Simulation, Programming Education, Human-Computer Interaction

**Abstract:**
This book delves into the realm of cognitive modeling and simulation in the context of prompt-based programming. It addresses the challenges in programming education, introduces the concept of prompt-based programming, and discusses the importance of cognitive modeling in enhancing programming learning efficiency and experience. The book is structured to provide a comprehensive guide, covering theoretical foundations, case studies, methodologies, and practical applications, aimed at both educators and developers seeking to improve the way programming is taught and learned.
