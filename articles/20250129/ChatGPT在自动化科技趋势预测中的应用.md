#### Mathematical Model and Formulas

The core of the algorithm is the Transformer model, which uses self-attention mechanisms to process and generate text. Mathematically, the self-attention mechanism can be represented as:

$$
\text{Attention}(Q, K, V) = \text{softmax}\left(\frac{QK^T}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right)V
$$

where:
- \(Q\), \(K\), and \(V\) are query, key, and value matrices, respectively.
- \(d_k\) is the dimension of the key vectors.
- The softmax function ensures that the attention weights sum to one.

The Transformer model consists of multiple layers, each of which contains self-attention mechanisms and feedforward neural networks. The overall model can be represented as:

$$
\text{Transformer}(X) = \text{Encoder}(X) = \text{LayerNorm}(X + \text{MultiHeadAttention}(X, X, X)) + X \\
\text{Decoder}(X) = \text{LayerNorm}(X + \text{MaskedMultiHeadAttention}(X, X, X) + \text{Feedforward}(X))
$$

where:
- \(X\) is the input sequence.
- \(\text{MultiHeadAttention}\) and \(\text{Feedforward}\) are the main components of the Transformer layer.
- \(\text{LayerNorm}\) and \(\text{MaskedMultiHeadAttention}\) are layer normalization and masked multi-head attention mechanisms, respectively.
