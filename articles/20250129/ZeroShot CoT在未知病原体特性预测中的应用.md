## Introduction

### 1.1 Problem Background

The emergence of unknown pathogens poses a significant threat to global public health. Traditional methods for predicting pathogen characteristics rely heavily on prior knowledge and extensive data sets. However, with the rise of antibiotic resistance and the rapid spread of emerging pathogens, there is an urgent need for more efficient and accurate prediction methods.

### 1.2 Zero-Shot Learning

Zero-Shot Learning (ZSL) is a branch of machine learning that aims to classify or predict properties of novel classes without any training examples of those classes. It is particularly useful in domains where obtaining labeled data for new classes is challenging or impossible.

### 1.3 CoT (Conceptualizers)

Conceptualizers, or CoT, are a type of deep learning model that can understand and process natural language descriptions of objects, their attributes, and relationships. CoT is capable of performing zero-shot learning by leveraging these descriptions to infer characteristics of unknown classes.

### 1.4 Importance of Predicting Unknown Pathogen Characteristics

Current methods for predicting pathogen characteristics are often limited by the availability of labeled data. Zero-Shot Learning and CoT offer a promising alternative by enabling the prediction of unknown pathogens based on their descriptions and relationships with known pathogens.

## Fundamental Concepts and Connections

### 2.1 Core Concepts of Zero-Shot Learning

- **Data Set Construction**: A key challenge in ZSL is the construction of a suitable data set that includes both seen and unseen classes. This often involves creating a synthetic data set or using transfer learning techniques.
- **Feature Extraction and Representation**: Extracting meaningful features from the data set is crucial for ZSL. Techniques such as transfer learning, metric learning, and attribute-based models are commonly used.
- **Zero-Shot Learning Algorithms**: Common ZSL algorithms include Prototypical Networks, Matching Networks, and Relation Network.
