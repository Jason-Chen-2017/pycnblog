#### Background and Challenges

Machine learning has witnessed exponential growth in recent years, with applications ranging from autonomous vehicles to natural language processing and healthcare. At the core of these applications are machine learning algorithms, which are designed to learn from data and make predictions or decisions. However, the success of these algorithms is highly dependent on their optimization.

**Optimization in Machine Learning**

Optimization in machine learning refers to the process of adjusting model parameters to minimize a loss function, thereby improving the model's performance. The optimization process is crucial as it determines how well the model can generalize to unseen data. Common optimization techniques include gradient descent, stochastic gradient descent, and more advanced methods like Adam and RMSprop.

**Challenges in Optimization**

Despite the advancements in optimization techniques, several challenges persist:
1. **Local Minima**: Many machine learning models are non-convex, which means they may get trapped in local minima instead of the global minimum.
2. **Computational Complexity**: As models grow in complexity and size, the computational cost of optimization algorithms increases significantly.
3. **Overfitting**: Optimized models may become overly complex, leading to overfitting, where they perform well on training data but poorly on unseen data.
4. **Scalability**: Optimizing models on large datasets requires efficient and scalable algorithms.
