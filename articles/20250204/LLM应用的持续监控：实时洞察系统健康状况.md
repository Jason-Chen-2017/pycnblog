### Summary:
In this comprehensive guide, we will delve into the world of continuous monitoring for LLM (Large Language Model) applications. By understanding the principles, technologies, and tools involved, we aim to equip you with the knowledge to effectively monitor and maintain the health of your LLM systems in real-time. This will not only ensure optimal performance but also provide valuable insights into potential issues before they escalate. Through practical case studies and advanced monitoring techniques, we will explore the future trends and challenges that lie ahead in this rapidly evolving field.
