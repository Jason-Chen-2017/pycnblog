2. **Self-Attention Masking**: To ensure self-consistency, the Core Transformer uses self-attention masking. This mechanism prevents the model from attending to parts of the input sequence that are not relevant to the current context, thus ensuring that the model's predictions are based on the most relevant information.

3. **Feed-Forward Neural Networks**: Between the self-attention and the normalization steps, the Core Transformer includes feed-forward neural networks. These networks help the model learn more complex representations of the input data and contribute to its overall performance.

4. **Normalization and Activation Functions**: The Core Transformer employs layer normalization and activation functions (such as ReLU) to stabilize the training process and improve convergence. These techniques help the model maintain its self-consistency properties by ensuring that the gradients do not vanish or explode during training.
