#### 3.1 Mathematical Model of AIGC

The mathematical model of AIGC is built upon the principles of machine learning and natural language processing. One of the most commonly used models is the Transformer model, which consists of several key components:

- **Encoder-Decoder Architecture**: The Transformer model employs an encoder-decoder architecture, where the encoder processes the input data and the decoder generates the output content.
- **Attention Mechanism**: The attention mechanism allows the model to focus on different parts of the input data, enabling it to generate contextually relevant content.
- **Positional Encoding**: Positional encodings are added to the input data to capture the spatial information of words within a sentence.

The Transformer model can be represented as follows:

$$
\text{Output} = \text{Decoder}(\text{Encoder}(\text{Input}) + \text{Positional Encoding})
$$

#### 3.2 Key Formulas and Their Significance

Several key formulas are used in the Transformer model to facilitate content generation:

- **Multi-head Self-Attention**:
$$
\text{Attention}(Q, K, V) = \text{softmax}\left(\frac{QK^T}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right)V
$$
This formula computes the self-attention scores for each word in the input sequence, allowing the model to weigh the importance of different words when generating the output.

- **Scaled Dot-Product Attention**:
$$
\text{ScaledDotProductAttention}(Q, K, V) = \text{softmax}\left(\frac{QK^T}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right)V
$$
This formula normalizes the attention scores using a scaling factor to prevent the attention mechanism from becoming too sensitive to the input data.

- **Positional Encoding**:
$$
\text{PE}(pos, 2i) = \sin\left(\frac{pos}{10000^{2i/d}}\right)
$$
$$
\text{PE}(pos, 2i+1) = \cos\left(\frac{pos}{10000^{2i/d}}\right)
$$
These formulas generate positional encodings to capture the spatial information of words within a sentence, enabling the model to understand the context and relationships between words.

The significance of these formulas lies in their ability to enhance the model's ability to generate highly personalized and contextually relevant content, which is crucial for effective talent retention strategies.
