## 1.5 Overview of Self-Consistency Confidence (Self-Consistency CoT)

### 1.5.1 Basic Concepts of Self-Consistency Confidence

Self-Consistency Confidence (CoT) is a relatively new concept that has gained attention in the field of quantum cryptography. It is rooted in quantum information theory, particularly the principles of quantum entanglement and superdense coding. The core idea behind CoT is to maintain the consistency of quantum information during transmission, thereby ensuring its integrity and accuracy.

### 1.5.2 Core Principles of Self-Consistency Confidence

The core principles of Self-Consistency Confidence are centered around quantum entanglement and superdense coding. Quantum entanglement allows for the creation of correlated quantum states that can be used to encode and transmit information. Superdense coding leverages this entanglement to transmit more information than what can be conveyed by classical methods.

### 1.5.3 Application Value of Self-Consistency Confidence in Quantum Cryptography

Self-Consistency Confidence offers significant value in quantum cryptography, particularly in enhancing system security and reliability:

- **Enhanced Security**: By maintaining self-consistency, CoT can effectively protect against quantum computer attacks, thereby increasing the security of quantum cryptographic systems.
- **Improved Reliability**: CoT can detect and correct errors in quantum information during transmission, enhancing the reliability of quantum cryptographic systems.
- **Performance Optimization**: CoT can optimize the performance of quantum cryptographic systems by reducing error rates and communication delays.
