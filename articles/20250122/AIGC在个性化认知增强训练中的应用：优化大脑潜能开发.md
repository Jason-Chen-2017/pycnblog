### Problem Background and Core Concepts

#### 1.1 Problem Background

Cognitive enhancement training aims to improve cognitive abilities by systematically training various cognitive functions. With the rapid development of information technology, traditional cognitive enhancement training methods, which often rely on repetitive exercises and fixed training materials, have shown limitations in meeting the diverse needs of individuals.

The emergence of AIGC provides a novel approach to personalized cognitive enhancement. By utilizing generative models, AIGC can generate tailored training materials that adapt to individual cognitive profiles, offering a more effective and engaging training experience.

#### 1.2 AIGC: Concept and Characteristics

AIGC is a subfield of artificial intelligence that focuses on generating new content based on existing data. The key characteristics of AIGC include:

1. **Data-driven**: AIGC leverages large-scale data to train generative models, ensuring the generated content is data-driven and representative of real-world scenarios.
2. **Personalization**: AIGC can generate personalized content that adapts to individual cognitive profiles, providing targeted training for different cognitive functions.
3. **Engagement**: By creating interactive and engaging training materials, AIGC can improve user engagement and motivation, leading to better training outcomes.

#### 1.3 AIGC in Cognitive Enhancement Training: Prospects

The application of AIGC in cognitive enhancement training has shown promising results. By generating personalized training materials, AIGC can effectively address the limitations of traditional training methods, offering a more effective and engaging approach to cognitive enhancement.

Furthermore, the continuous improvement of AIGC algorithms and the increasing availability of large-scale data sources will further enhance the capabilities of AIGC in cognitive enhancement training. This will lead to the development of more sophisticated and personalized cognitive training systems, revolutionizing the field of cognitive enhancement.
