**Title**: Kierkegaard and Nietzsche: Pioneers of Existentialism and Nihilism

**Keywords**: Existentialism, Nihilism, Kierkegaard, Nietzsche, Philosophy

**Abstract**:
This book delves into the philosophies of Søren Kierkegaard and Friedrich Nietzsche, two seminal thinkers who revolutionized our understanding of existence and non-existence. In the 19th century, the world witnessed the rise of two distinct but interconnected philosophical movements: existentialism and nihilism. These movements challenged traditional notions of morality, truth, and human purpose, leading to profound shifts in the way individuals perceive their lives and the world around them. Kierkegaard and Nietzsche, often regarded as the architects of these movements, offer unique perspectives that continue to shape contemporary philosophical thought.
