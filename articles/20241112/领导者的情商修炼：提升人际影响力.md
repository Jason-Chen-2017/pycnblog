To give readers a quick overview of the key themes covered in this book, we will highlight the following core keywords:

5. **Social Skills**: Building positive relationships is essential for leadership success. This section will cover the development of effective communication skills, trust-building techniques, and conflict resolution strategies.

6. **Motivation**: Leaders play a pivotal role in motivating their teams. This chapter will explore various motivational theories and practices, providing leaders with tools to inspire and empower their team members.

7. **Leadership Effectiveness**: The ultimate goal of this book is to help leaders enhance their effectiveness through the development of EQ. This section will provide a comprehensive overview of how EQ relates to key leadership outcomes, such as decision-making, team building, and conflict resolution.
