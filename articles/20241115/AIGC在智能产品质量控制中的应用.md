## Chapter 1 Introduction to AIGC and Intelligent Product Quality Control

### 1.1 Overview of AIGC
#### 1.1.1 Definition of AIGC
- **AIGC**: Auto Generated Content technology, which uses deep learning models to automatically generate texts, images, audios, etc.

#### 1.1.2 Core Concepts and Relationships
- **Generating Models**:
  - **GPT**: Generates text, such as articles and conversations.
  - **DALL-E**: Generates images.
  - **Other Generating Models**: Like GLM, PaLM, etc., which generate content with specific conditions.

- **Conditional Generating Models**:
  - **GLM**: Generates content based on the language model with conditional information.
  - **PaLM**: A large-scale pre-trained language model that supports complex conditional generation.

### 1.2 Overview of Intelligent Product Quality Control
#### 1.2.1 Definition
- **Intelligent Product Quality Control**: Utilizes AI technology to monitor and control the quality of products in the production process.

#### 1.2.2 Core Concepts and Relationships
- **Data Collection and Processing**:
  - **Sensor Data Collection**: Collects data generated during the production process.
  - **Data Processing**: Cleans and normalizes the collected data.

- **Machine Learning Algorithms**:
  - **Pattern Recognition Algorithms**: Such as Support Vector Machines (SVM), Decision Trees, etc.
  - **Prediction Algorithms**: Such as Linear Regression, Neural Networks, etc.

- **Intelligent Decision Support**:
  - **Automatically Adjusting Production Parameters**: Adjusts production equipment parameters based on product quality prediction results.
  - **Optimizing Production Processes**: Analyzes production data to optimize production processes.

### 1.3 Application Prospects of AIGC in Intelligent Product Quality Control
#### 1.3.1 Application Fields
- **Quality Control Warning**: Real-time monitoring of product defects, early warning.
- **Quality Assessment and Optimization**: Automatically assess product quality, make optimization suggestions.
- **Production Process Optimization**: Analyze production data to optimize production processes.
