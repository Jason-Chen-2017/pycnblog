### Step 2: Fundamental Concepts and Principles

**Chapter 2: Fundamental Concepts and Principles of Prompt Design**

**2.1 Definition and Types of Prompts**

- **Definition:**
  - A prompt is a cue or stimulus that guides an AI model to generate a desired output or response.
  - Types of prompts: Open-ended, Closed-ended, Yes/No, List, and Fill-in-the-Blank.

**2.2 Design Principles**

- **Principles:**
  - Clarity: The prompt should be clear and easy to understand.
  - Brevity: The prompt should be concise to avoid confusion.
  - Emotional Appeal: The prompt should evoke a desired emotional response.
  - Scalability: The prompt should be adaptable for varying contexts and users.
