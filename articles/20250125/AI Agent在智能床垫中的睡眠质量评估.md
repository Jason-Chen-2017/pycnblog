## Introduction to AI Agents

### Chapter 1: Basic Concepts and Principles of AI Agents

**1.1 Definition and Types of AI Agents**
AI agents are intelligent entities that can perceive their environment, make decisions, and take actions to achieve specific goals. They can be broadly classified into two types: reactive agents and goal-based agents. Reactive agents respond to specific stimuli in their environment without any memory of past events, while goal-based agents have a set of predefined goals and make decisions to achieve those goals.

**1.2 Working Principles of AI Agents**
AI agents work through a series of well-defined processes. First, they perceive the environment through sensors. Then, they process the sensory information to understand the current state of the environment. Based on this understanding, they make decisions using a decision-making module. Finally, they execute the decisions through actuators.

**1.3 Core Components of AI Agents**
The core components of AI agents include the perception module, decision-making module, action module, and learning module. The perception module gathers information from the environment. The decision-making module processes the information and generates actions. The action module executes the actions, and the learning module allows the agent to improve its performance over time.

**1.4 Applications of AI Agents**
AI agents have a wide range of applications, including in smart homes, health monitoring, and smart healthcare. In smart homes, AI agents can control various devices and systems to improve comfort and energy efficiency. In health monitoring, AI agents can track vital signs and detect health issues. In smart healthcare, AI agents can assist doctors in making diagnoses and treatment decisions.

## Overview of Smart Mattress Technology

### Chapter 2: Basic Structure of Smart Mattresses

**2.1 Hardware Composition of Smart Mattresses**
Smart mattresses are equipped with various sensors that collect data on the sleeper's movements, temperature, and pressure. These sensors are connected to a communication module that transmits the data to a central processing unit. The processing unit then analyzes the data to provide insights into the sleeper's sleep patterns and quality.

**2.2 Software System Architecture**
The software system of a smart mattress consists of several components. Data collection and preprocessing occur at the sensor level. The processed data is then transmitted to a central database for storage and analysis. The analysis results are used to provide feedback and adjustments to the mattress settings.

**2.3 Development History of Smart Mattresses**
Smart mattresses have evolved from simple, manual adjustments to fully automated, adaptive systems. Early smart mattresses had limited capabilities and relied on manual adjustments. Modern smart mattresses use advanced sensors and AI algorithms to provide personalized comfort and support.
