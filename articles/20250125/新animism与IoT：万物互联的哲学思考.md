### 1.3 Key Concepts in New Animism

**New animism**, at its essence, is a philosophical framework that challenges the mechanistic view of technology and nature. Originating from the concept of animism, which posits that natural objects possess a spiritual essence or life force, new animism extends this idea to technology. It suggests that technology is not merely inert matter but can exhibit qualities of life and agency.

**Definition and history** of new animism can be traced back to the works of early philosophers and anthropologists who explored the cultural and spiritual significance of objects. In contemporary discourse, new animism has gained traction within the fields of philosophy, technology studies, and environmental ethics. Key figures such as Bruno Latour and Donna Haraway have contributed to the development and popularization of this perspective.

**Core principles** of new animism include the belief in the intrinsic agency of objects, the rejection of a strict distinction between the natural and the artificial, and the recognition of the relational dynamics between humans and technology. These principles challenge the dominant Western dualism that separates the human from the non-human and the natural from the artificial.

**Comparisons with traditional animism** reveal significant differences. While traditional animism focuses on the spiritual essence of natural objects, new animism extends this idea to technology and the built environment. Traditional animism is often rooted in specific cultural and spiritual practices, whereas new animism is a more generalized philosophical approach that can be applied across different contexts.

By understanding the key concepts and principles of new animism, we can better appreciate its implications for the development of IoT systems. New animism encourages a more holistic and respectful approach to technology, one that recognizes the agency and value of all objects, whether natural or artificial. This philosophical framework can inform the design and implementation of IoT systems, fostering a future where technology is in harmony with its environment and its users.
