## Key Concepts and Relationships in AI-driven Knowledge Graphs

### Core Concept of AI-driven Knowledge Graphs

AI-driven knowledge graphs are structures that combine artificial intelligence with semantic networks to model and represent the knowledge of an organization in a structured, machine-readable form. They leverage data from various sources, integrate it with domain-specific knowledge, and enable advanced analytics and decision-making.

### Core Concepts and Their Relationships

#### 1. Knowledge Graph
A knowledge graph is a semantic network that represents entities and their relationships in a structured format. It uses nodes to represent entities and edges to represent relationships between these entities. 

#### 2. Entity
Entities are objects or concepts that are represented in the knowledge graph. They can be people, places, events, or any other concept relevant to the domain.

#### 3. Relationship
Relationships are the connections between entities. They describe how entities are related to each other and can be directed or undirected.

#### 4. Attribute
Attributes are additional properties or characteristics associated with entities. They provide more detailed information about the entities and their relationships.

#### 5. Edge Weight
Edge weight represents the strength or importance of a relationship between entities. It can be used to prioritize or filter the relationships in the graph.

#### 6. Graph Database
A graph database is a type of database that stores data using a graph structure with nodes, edges, and attributes. It is used to efficiently store and query knowledge graphs.
