To start, we'll delve into the basics of Knowledge Graph and reasoning. Knowledge Graph is a powerful data structure that represents real-world entities and their relationships. It is widely used in various applications, including semantic search, recommendation systems, and natural language understanding.

**Background:**

1. **Definition and Role of Knowledge Graph**: A Knowledge Graph is essentially a graph data structure that consists of nodes (entities), edges (relationships), and attributes. It captures the relationships between entities in a structured and machine-readable format.
2. **Historical Development**: The concept of Knowledge Graphs has evolved over time, with early examples coming from the fields of database and information retrieval. However, the modern approach, heavily influenced by the Web, has gained prominence in recent years.
3. **Components and Structure**: Knowledge Graphs can be composed of various components, such as entities, relationships, properties, and types. Understanding these components is crucial for building effective Knowledge Graphs.
