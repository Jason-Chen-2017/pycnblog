#### 4.1 Algorithm Overview

The Self-Consistency CoT algorithm operates based on a set of principles that ensure the system's outputs remain consistent and accurate over time. The key components of the algorithm include:

1. **Input Data Processing:** The algorithm starts by processing the input data, which could be a variety of data types depending on the application.
2. **Context Extraction:** The algorithm extracts contextual information from the input data to understand the current environment and conditions.
3. **Output Generation:** Based on the processed input data and extracted context, the algorithm generates an initial output.
4. **Consistency Check:** The algorithm then checks the consistency of the output by comparing it with the system's internal knowledge and previous outputs.
5. **Adjustment:** If the output is inconsistent, the algorithm makes adjustments to the output to improve its consistency.
6. **Feedback Loop:** The adjusted output is fed back into the system to update the internal knowledge and context, and the process repeats.

#### 4.4 Mathematical Model and Formulas

The Self-Consistency CoT algorithm is grounded in a mathematical model that helps explain how the system adjusts its outputs. The core formulas include:

1. **Consistency Measure (CM):**
   $$ CM = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n} |O_i - O_{i-1}|}{n} $$
   where \( CM \) is the consistency measure, \( O_i \) is the current output, and \( O_{i-1} \) is the previous output. The consistency measure quantifies the difference between consecutive outputs.

2. **Adjustment Factor (AF):**
   $$ AF = \frac{CM - \text{threshold}}{\text{max\_CM} - \text{threshold}} $$
   where \( AF \) is the adjustment factor, \( CM \) is the consistency measure, and \( \text{threshold} \) is a predefined threshold. The adjustment factor determines the extent of the adjustment needed.

3. **Adjusted Output (AO):**
   $$ AO = O_i + AF \times (O_{i-1} - O_i) $$
   where \( AO \) is the adjusted output, \( O_i \) is the current output, and \( O_{i-1} \) is the previous output. The adjusted output is calculated based on the adjustment factor.
