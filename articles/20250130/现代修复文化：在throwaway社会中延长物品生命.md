### Step 1: Introduction to Modern Repair Culture

**Background:**
In the era of consumerism and rapid technological advancement, the culture of repairing objects has been overshadowed by the convenience and low cost of replacement. However, this throwaway society leads to significant waste and environmental degradation. Modern repair culture aims to address these issues by emphasizing the importance of repairing and maintaining objects rather than discarding them.

**Concept Explanation:**
Repair culture encompasses the principles, practices, and attitudes that promote the repair of objects over disposal. It involves a shift from a linear model of production and consumption to a circular model that values longevity and sustainability.

**Issue Definition:**
The problem lies in the lack of awareness and knowledge about the benefits of repairing, as well as the degradation of skills related to repair work. This leads to a situation where most items are thrown away prematurely, resulting in increased waste and resource depletion.

**Solution Approach:**
The solution involves fostering a repair culture through education, policy support, and the development of innovative repair technologies.
