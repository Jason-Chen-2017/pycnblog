#### Abstract:
In this comprehensive guide, we delve into the transformative potential of augmented reality (AR) in fostering interactive reading experiences for parents and children. We begin by introducing the concept of digital parent-child reading clubs and their significance in the modern era. Following this, we explore the fundamentals of AR, its benefits, and theoretical underpinnings in the context of reading. The article then transitions to practical design principles and user experience considerations for AR reading applications. Case studies and practical implementations are discussed to illustrate the real-world application of AR in reading. The article concludes with a summary of best practices and future directions for enhancing interactive reading experiences.
