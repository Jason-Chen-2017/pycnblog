### Background and Core Concepts

**1.1 Large Model-driven Demand Analysis**

Demand analysis is a crucial step in software development that involves identifying and understanding the needs and requirements of stakeholders. Traditionally, this process has been manual and time-consuming, often resulting in incomplete or inaccurate requirements. Large model-driven demand analysis leverages the power of artificial intelligence, particularly large language models, to automate and enhance the demand analysis process.

**1.2 Prompt Conversion**

Prompt conversion refers to the process of transforming user input (prompts) into structured data that can be easily analyzed and acted upon by a computer system. This is particularly important in scenarios where user input is unstructured and needs to be processed by automated systems.
