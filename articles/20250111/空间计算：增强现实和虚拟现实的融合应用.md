## Introduction to Spatial Computing and AR/VR

### 1.1.1 The Concept and Importance of Spatial Computing

Spatial computing refers to the use of data and technology to represent and interact with the world in three dimensions. The importance of spatial computing lies in its ability to revolutionize how we interact with digital information and the physical world. ### 1.1.2 Definitions of Augmented Reality and Virtual Reality

Augmented Reality (AR) is an interactive experience of a real-world environment where the objects that reside in the real world are augmented by computer-generated sensory information. This can include sounds, images, videos, and other sensory enhancements. AR enhances our perception of reality, making it more engaging and informative.

Virtual Reality (VR), on the other hand, is an immersive digital environment that completely replaces the real-world with a simulated one. Users are surrounded by a virtual environment and can interact with it through sensory input and motion tracking.
