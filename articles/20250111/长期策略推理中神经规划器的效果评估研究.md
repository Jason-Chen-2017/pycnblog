                 



### 1.1 Research Background

#### Problem Background

Long-term strategic reasoning is a critical component in various domains, including economics, artificial intelligence (AI), and decision-making. The ability to foresee the long-term consequences of current actions is essential for optimizing outcomes and achieving long-term success. In the realm of AI, the complexity and variability of real-world problems necessitate the development of advanced techniques that can handle long-term strategic reasoning.

#### Problem Description

Current methods in long-term strategic reasoning face several challenges and limitations. Traditional planning algorithms, such as those based on classical logic and search techniques, are often inadequate for dealing with the large state spaces and uncertainties inherent in many real-world problems. Additionally, these methods struggle with the exponential growth of planning horizons, making them impractical for long-term planning.

#### Problem Solution

Neural planners, a relatively new approach, show promising potential in addressing these challenges. By leveraging the power of deep learning and neural networks, neural planners can model complex relationships and learn from large amounts of data, enabling more effective long-term strategic reasoning. The integration of neural planners with reinforcement learning and other machine learning techniques further enhances their ability to handle uncertainty and adapt to changing environments.

#### Boundary and Extension

The research will focus on the effectiveness evaluation of neural planners in long-term strategic reasoning. The scope will include a comprehensive analysis of various neural planner architectures and their performance in different problem domains. The findings and insights from this research will also be relevant to other fields that require long-term strategic reasoning, such as robotics, autonomous driving, and finance.

### 1.2 Core Concepts

#### Concept 1: Neural Planner

##### Definition

A neural planner is an AI system that utilizes neural networks to generate plans or decision sequences for achieving specific goals. It learns from historical data or experiences to predict the future states of the environment and make optimal decisions.

##### Core Characteristics

- **Data-driven**: Neural planners learn from data, allowing them to adapt to changing environments and new situations.
- **Contextual**: They can generate context-aware plans, taking into account the current state of the environment and the desired goals.
- **Generalization**: Neural planners can generalize their knowledge to new, unseen scenarios, making them suitable for long-term strategic reasoning.

##### Comparison with Traditional Planning Methods

| Feature | Neural Planner | Traditional Planner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flexibility | High | Low |
| Adaptability | High | Low |
| Learning Ability | High | Low |
| Scalability | High | Low |

#### Concept 2: Long-term Strategic Reasoning

##### Definition

Long-term strategic reasoning is the process of planning and making decisions that consider the long-term consequences of current actions. It involves anticipating future events, evaluating potential outcomes, and choosing the best course of action to achieve long-term goals.

##### Importance in Decision-making

Effective long-term strategic reasoning is crucial for making informed decisions that lead to optimal outcomes. It enables organizations and individuals to anticipate challenges, seize opportunities, and adapt to changing circumstances.

##### Challenges in Implementation

- **Complexity**: Real-world problems are often complex and dynamic, making it difficult to predict their long-term outcomes accurately.
- **Uncertainty**: The future is uncertain, and predictions based on past data may not hold true in the future.
- **Resource Constraints**: Long-term strategic reasoning requires significant computational resources and time.
