## 1. Introduction to Edge Computing and Autonomous Driving

### 1.1 Background

- **Edge Computing**: A paradigm shifting from centralized cloud computing to decentralized computing at the network edge.
- **Autonomous Driving**: The development of vehicles that can navigate without human input.
- **Low-Latency Processing**: The need for real-time decision-making and control in autonomous vehicles.

### 1.2 Challenges

- **Data Processing Delays**: Autonomous vehicles generate massive amounts of data that need to be processed quickly.
- **Network Latency**: Relying solely on cloud computing can introduce significant delays in data processing.

### 1.3 Importance of Low-Latency Processing

- **Safety Critical**: Rapid decision-making is crucial for the safety of passengers and other road users.
- **Improved Efficiency**: Reduced latency leads to better vehicle performance and energy efficiency.
