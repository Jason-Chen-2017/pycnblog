#### 1.1 Research Background and Significance

**Current State of Language Acquisition Research**

Language acquisition has been a topic of significant interest in cognitive science and psychology for decades. Traditional research has focused on various aspects of language learning, such as phonology, syntax, semantics, and pragmatics. However, recent advancements in artificial intelligence (AI) have opened new avenues for exploring how language acquisition can be enhanced through technological interventions.

**Importance of Cognitive Neural Plasticity in Language Learning**

Cognitive neural plasticity refers to the brain's ability to change and reorganize itself in response to new experiences, environmental challenges, and learning opportunities. This concept is crucial in understanding how individuals learn languages and adapt to new linguistic environments. Language learning involves complex cognitive processes that rely on neural plasticity, which allows the brain to reorganize its neural circuits to facilitate language acquisition.

#### 1.2 ChatGPT: A Brief Overview

**What is ChatGPT?**

ChatGPT is a state-of-the-art language model developed by OpenAI. It is based on a deep learning architecture known as the Transformer, which has shown remarkable performance in natural language processing tasks. ChatGPT is capable of generating coherent and contextually relevant text, making it a valuable tool for language learning and research.

**Capabilities and Limitations of ChatGPT in Language Learning**

ChatGPT's ability to process and generate language can be leveraged to facilitate language learning in several ways. It can provide instant feedback, generate tailored language exercises, and engage in meaningful conversations that mimic real-life interactions. However, it is essential to acknowledge the limitations of ChatGPT, such as its reliance on large amounts of text data for training and its potential to produce inaccurate or nonsensical responses.

#### 1.3 Neural Plasticity and Language Acquisition

**Basic Concepts of Neural Plasticity**

Neural plasticity refers to the brain's ability to modify its structure and function in response to learning, experience, and neural activity. This concept encompasses various processes, including synaptic plasticity, structural plasticity, and neural regeneration.

**Role of Neural Plasticity in Language Learning and Brain Remodeling**

Neural plasticity plays a critical role in language learning by allowing the brain to adapt to new linguistic patterns and structures. This process involves the reorganization of neural circuits, which enables individuals to acquire new languages and refine their language skills over time.
