#### Introduction

Energy-efficient computing is an emerging field that aims to optimize the use of computational resources while minimizing energy consumption. As the IT industry continues to grow at an unprecedented rate, the carbon footprint of data centers and other computing facilities has become a significant concern. This book, "Energy-efficient Computing: Addressing the IT Carbon Footprint Challenge with New Technologies," provides a comprehensive guide to understanding and implementing energy-efficient computing solutions.

The book is divided into several key sections:

1. **Introduction**: This section sets the stage by providing an overview of the problem of IT carbon footprint and the importance of energy-efficient computing.
2. **Core Concepts and Technologies**: Here, we delve into the core concepts and technologies that underpin energy-efficient computing, such as green data centers, virtualization, and energy management systems.
3. **Algorithm Principles and Explanations**: This section explains the mathematical models and algorithms used in energy-efficient computing, with visual aids to enhance understanding.
4. **System Analysis and Architecture Design**: We explore how to design and analyze energy-efficient systems, including considerations for hardware, software, and network architectures.
5. **Practical Projects and Case Studies**: Real-world examples and case studies illustrate the application of energy-efficient computing in various industries and scenarios.
6. **Best Practices and Summary**: This section summarizes the key takeaways and provides best practices for implementing energy-efficient computing solutions.
7. **Additional Resources**: Finally, we offer additional resources for further learning and exploration.

By following these steps, readers will gain a deep understanding of energy-efficient computing and be equipped to address the IT carbon footprint challenge effectively.
