### Algorithm and Mathematical Models for Micro-Holiday House Design

#### 3.1 Overview of Design Algorithms

**Step 1: Basic Algorithms for Space Planning**

Effective space planning is crucial in the design of micro-holiday houses, where every square foot counts. Several basic algorithms can be employed to optimize space utilization. One such algorithm is the "Space Allocation Algorithm," which ensures that the available space is distributed efficiently among various functional areas. This algorithm works by prioritizing the allocation of space based on the importance and size of each functional area.

**Algorithm: Space Allocation Algorithm**

1. **Input:** List of functional areas and their required space.
2. **Output:** Optimized space allocation plan.

3.1.1 **Pseudocode:**

```
function SpaceAllocation(functional_areas):
    sort functional_areas by required_space in descending order
    allocated_space = []
    total_space = available_space

    for area in functional_areas:
        if total_space >= area.required_space:
            allocated_space.append(area)
            total_space -= area.required_space

    return allocated_space
```

**Step 2: Optimization Algorithms for Minimalist Design**

Minimalist design aims to create efficient and aesthetically pleasing spaces. Optimization algorithms can be used to refine the space allocation plan and enhance the overall design. One popular optimization algorithm is the "Genetic Algorithm," which mimics the process of natural selection to find optimal solutions. This algorithm is particularly useful for solving complex design problems where multiple constraints need to be considered.

**Algorithm: Genetic Algorithm**

1. **Input:** Initial population of design solutions.
2. **Output:** Optimized design solution.

3.2.1 **Pseudocode:**

```
function GeneticAlgorithm(population, fitness_function, max_iterations):
    for iteration in 1 to max_iterations:
        new_population = []

        for individual in population:
            new_population.append(mutate(individual))
            new_population.append(crossover(population.random_individual()))

        population = new_population
        population = select_best_individuals(population, fitness_function)

    return best_individual(population)
```

#### 3.2 Mathematical Models and Formulas

**Step 1: Space Allocation Model**

The Space Allocation Model is a mathematical framework used to determine the optimal distribution of space among different functional areas. This model considers the total available space, the required space for each functional area, and the constraints of the design.

**Mathematical Model: Space Allocation Model**

Let \( S \) be the total available space, \( A_i \) be the required space for the \( i \)-th functional area, and \( x_i \) be the actual allocated space for the \( i \)-th functional area. The model can be expressed as:

$$
\begin{align*}
\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i &\leq S \\
x_i &\geq 0 \quad \forall i
\end{align*}
$$

where \( n \) is the number of functional areas.

**Step 2: Comfort and Efficiency Model**

Comfort and efficiency are critical factors in micro-holiday house design. The Comfort and Efficiency Model uses a combination of qualitative and quantitative metrics to evaluate the design based on comfort and efficiency. This model considers factors such as lighting, ventilation, and ergonomics.

**Mathematical Model: Comfort and Efficiency Model**

Let \( C \) be the comfort score, \( E \) be the efficiency score, and \( w_c \) and \( w_e \) be the weights assigned to comfort and efficiency, respectively. The model can be expressed as:

$$
\begin{align*}
C &= w_c \cdot C_{lighting} + w_c \cdot C_{ventilation} + w_c \cdot C_{ergonomics} \\
E &= w_e \cdot E_{sustainability} + w_e \cdot E_{space_efficiency} + w_e \cdot E_{user_experience}
\end{align*}
$$

where \( C_{lighting} \), \( C_{ventilation} \), and \( C_{ergonomics} \) are the comfort scores for lighting, ventilation, and ergonomics, and \( E_{sustainability} \), \( E_{space_efficiency} \), and \( E_{user_experience} \) are the efficiency scores for sustainability, space efficiency, and user experience.

   def optimize_space_allocation(functional_areas, available_space):
       design_plan = DesignPlan.objects.create()
       allocated_space = available_space

       for functional_area in functional_areas:
           if allocated_space >= functional_area.required_space:
               SpaceAllocation.objects.create(design_plan=design_plan, functional_area=functional_area, required_space=functional_area.required_space, allocated_space=functional_area.required_space)

           else:
               SpaceAllocation.objects.create(design_plan=design_plan, functional_area=functional_area, required_space=functional_area.required_space, allocated_space=allocated_space)
               break

       return design_plan
