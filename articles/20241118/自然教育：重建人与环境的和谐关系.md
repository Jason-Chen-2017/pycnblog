### Mathematical Models and Formulas
Mathematical models and formulas can be used to explain the relationship between human activities and environmental impacts. For instance, the formula for calculating the ecological footprint can be presented as:
$$\text{Ecological Footprint} = \frac{\text{Total Human Claim}}{\text{Biologically Productive Area}}$$

### Detailed Explanation and Example
The ecological footprint measures the amount of biologically productive land and water area required to produce the resources a human population consumes and to absorb the wastes they generate, based on prevailing technology. For example, if a country's ecological footprint is 2.5 global hectares (gha) per capita, and its biologically productive area is only 1.5 gha per capita, it has a deficit of 1 gha per capita.
