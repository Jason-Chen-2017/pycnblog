**Introduction to Ocean Resource Exploration**

Ocean resource exploration is a critical area of study that seeks to discover and map the vast resources present in the world's oceans. These resources include:

- **Geological Resources**: Such as oil, natural gas, minerals, and offshore wind energy.
- **Biological Resources**: Including fish stocks, marine plants, and other living organisms.
- **Environmental Resources**: Such as water quality, temperature, and salinity.

The importance of ocean resource exploration lies in its potential to:

- **Economic Development**: By providing resources that can be harvested and traded.
- **Scientific Research**: By contributing to our understanding of marine ecosystems and processes.
- **National Security**: By ensuring access to strategic resources and monitoring potential threats.

**Core Concepts and Theoretical Framework**

Artificial intelligence (AI) is the simulation of human intelligence in machines, particularly computer systems. Machine learning (ML), a subset of AI, enables computers to learn from data, identify patterns, and make decisions with minimal human intervention.

In the context of ocean resource exploration, the core concepts and theoretical frameworks include:

- **Supervised Learning**: Where models are trained on labeled data to predict outcomes.
- **Unsupervised Learning**: Where models discover hidden patterns or intrinsic structures in unlabeled data.
- **Reinforcement Learning**: Where models learn optimal behaviors through interactions with an environment.
