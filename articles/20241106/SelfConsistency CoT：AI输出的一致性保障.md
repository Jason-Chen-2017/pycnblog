#### 2.1.1 Data Consistency Detection Algorithms

Data consistency detection algorithms are designed to identify and verify the consistency of data within an AI system.

$$
\text{function DataConsistencyCheck(data\_set)}
\begin{align*}
\qquad \text{for} \; i = 1 \; \text{to} \; n \\
\qquad \qquad \text{if} \; data\_set[i] \neq \text{Expected\_Value} \\
\qquad \qquad \qquad \text{return} \; \text{False} \\
\text{return} \; \text{True}
\end{align*}
$$

### 2.1.2 Logical Consistency Assurance Algorithms

Logical consistency detection and repair algorithms are used to ensure that the logical structure of an AI system remains consistent.

$$
\text{function LogicalConsistencyCheck(statement)}
\begin{align*}
\qquad \text{if} \; \neg(\text{statement1} \land \text{statement2}) \\
\qquad \qquad \text{return} \; \text{False} \\
\qquad \text{if} \; (\text{statement1} \land \neg\text{statement2}) \lor (\neg\text{statement1} \land \text{statement2}) \\
\qquad \qquad \text{return} \; \text{False} \\
\qquad \text{else} \\
\qquad \qquad \text{return} \; \text{True}
\end{align*}
$$
