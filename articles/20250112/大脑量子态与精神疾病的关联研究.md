### Keywords: Quantum States, Brain, Mental Disorders, Neuroscience, Research

### Abstract:
This book delves into the emerging field of neuroscience where quantum states of the brain are being investigated for their potential association with mental disorders. By presenting a comprehensive overview of current research, this book aims to bridge the gap between quantum physics and clinical psychology, providing insights into the fundamental mechanisms of mental health and disease. Through a systematic approach, it explores the theoretical frameworks, experimental methodologies, and potential applications of quantum neuroscience in understanding and treating mental illnesses.
