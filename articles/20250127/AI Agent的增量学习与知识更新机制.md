### 2. Overview of Incremental Learning Principles

#### 2.1 Necessity of Incremental Learning
AI systems often encounter new data and tasks continuously, making incremental learning essential for their adaptability and efficiency.

#### 2.2 Basic Principles of Incremental Learning
- Incremental learning algorithms are designed to update the model incrementally without retraining from scratch.
- They maintain the knowledge acquired during previous learning phases.

#### 2.3 Advantages and Challenges
- Advantages: Efficiency, Reduced data storage, Continuous improvement.
- Challenges: Overfitting, Accuracy degradation, Handling concept drift.

### 3. Discussion on Knowledge Update Mechanisms

#### 3.1 Definition and Importance of Knowledge Update

#### 3.2 Traditional Methods of Knowledge Update

#### 3.3 Modern Methods of Knowledge Update
