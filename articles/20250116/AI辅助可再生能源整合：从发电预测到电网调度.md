## Part 1: Introduction to the Background of Renewable Energy Integration

### 1.1 Problem Background

In an era marked by climate change and the depletion of fossil fuels, the world is increasingly turning to renewable energy sources such as solar, wind, hydro, and geothermal. However, the variability and intermittency of these sources pose significant challenges to grid stability and efficient energy management.

### 1.2 Problem Description

The integration of renewable energy sources into the power grid requires precise power forecasting and effective grid scheduling. Inaccurate forecasts can lead to energy shortages or surpluses, while inefficient scheduling can result in increased costs and reduced grid reliability.

### 1.3 Problem Solution

The application of AI in renewable energy integration addresses these challenges by enhancing power forecasting accuracy and optimizing grid scheduling. AI algorithms can process vast amounts of data, identify patterns, and make predictions with high precision.
