- **3.2.1 增长率计算公式**  
  $$\text{增长率} = \frac{\text{Earnings}_{\text{Next\_Year}} - \text{Earnings}_{\text{Current\_Year}}}{\text{Earnings}_{\text{Current\_Year}}}$$

- **3.2.2 估值模型**  
  $$\text{内在价值} = \frac{\text{Earnings}_{\text{Next\_Year}}}{\text{增长率} - \text{贴现率}}$$

- **3.2.3 风险评估模型**  
  $$\text{风险系数} = \beta \times \text{市场风险溢价}$$
