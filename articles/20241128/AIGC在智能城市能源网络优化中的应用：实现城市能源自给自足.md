#### 1.2 Overview of Smart Urban Energy Networks

- **1.2.1 Concept and Architecture of Smart Urban Energy Networks**
  - **Definition:**
    - A system that integrates multiple energy sources (e.g., solar, wind, nuclear) into a unified network.
  - **Architecture:**
    - **Layered Structure:**
      - Data collection and monitoring.

      - Control and management.
