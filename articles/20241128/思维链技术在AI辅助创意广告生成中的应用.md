Computer vision is another critical component of AI-assisted creative advertising. It enables AI to process and analyze visual information, such as images and videos. Computer vision can be used to generate visual content, detect emotions, and identify objects and scenes.

**Mathematical Model and Formula:**

$$

#### **4.3.3 Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs)**

GANs are a type of AI model that consists of two neural networks, a generator, and a discriminator. The generator creates visual content, while the discriminator evaluates the quality of the generated content. Through iterative training, the generator improves its ability to generate realistic and creative content.

**Mathematical Model and Formula:**

$$
\text{GAN Model} = \text{Generator} \xrightarrow{\text{Discriminator}} \text{Adversarial Training} \xrightarrow{\text{Improvement}} \text{Output}
$$
