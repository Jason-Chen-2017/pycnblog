### Abstract
This book delves into the intricacies of multilingual simultaneous translation systems and their evaluation. It explores the fundamental concepts, key technologies, and evaluation metrics essential for assessing the performance of these systems. With a focus on structured thinking and logical analysis, the book aims to provide a comprehensive understanding of the field, highlighting the latest advancements and their societal impact.
