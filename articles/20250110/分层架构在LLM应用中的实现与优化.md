#### Abstract:
This book delves into the intricacies of layered architecture, focusing on its application and optimization in the context of Large Language Models (LLM). As the field of artificial intelligence continues to advance, the importance of structured and efficient architectures cannot be overstated. This book aims to provide a comprehensive guide, exploring both theoretical underpinnings and practical applications. From understanding the core concepts and principles of layered architecture to its implementation and optimization in LLMs, this book covers all aspects. Whether you are a seasoned AI professional or a beginner looking to dive into this field, this book is your go-to resource for mastering layered architecture in LLM applications.
