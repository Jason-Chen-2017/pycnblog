**Chapter 1: Overview of Multi-modal LLM**

**Section 1.1: Introduction to Multi-modal and LLM Basics**

**1.1.1 Multi-modal Technology Overview:**
Multi-modal technology involves the integration of multiple types of data, such as text, images, audio, and video, to enhance the understanding and processing of information. In the context of LLMs, this means combining language data with other modalities to improve the model's performance in various tasks.

**1.1.2 Concept and Classification of Language Models (LLM):**
Language models are AI systems designed to understand and generate human language. There are various types of LLMs, including Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs), Transformer models, and GPT-based models, each with its unique characteristics and use cases.

**1.1.3 Importance and Application Scenarios of Multi-modal LLM:**
Multi-modal LLMs offer significant advantages in tasks that require understanding and generating language in conjunction with other forms of data. These applications include but are not limited to, question-answering systems, chatbots, and natural language understanding (NLU) in virtual assistants.
