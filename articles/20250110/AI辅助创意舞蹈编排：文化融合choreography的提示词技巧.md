#### 1.1.1 The Background of Dance Development and Needs

The development of dance has a long history, from ancient times to the present. With the continuous evolution of society and culture, dance has become not only a form of artistic expression but also a cultural communication tool. As technology advances, especially AI, there is a growing need for innovative ways to create and perform dance, as well as to integrate different cultural elements.

**Problems in Traditional Dance Creation and Performance**

1. **Lack of Creativity:** Traditional dance creation often relies heavily on traditional methods and styles, which may limit the creativity of choreographers.
2. **Time-consuming:** The process of creating a new dance piece from scratch can be time-consuming and requires a lot of manual labor.
3. **Limited Cultural Integration:** Traditional dance often represents a single culture, making it difficult to integrate various cultural elements in a meaningful way.

#### 1.1.2 AI Applications in Artistic Creation

Artistic creation, including dance, has seen significant advancements with the integration of AI. AI can analyze large amounts of data, identify patterns, and generate new ideas, thus providing a new approach to artistic creation.

**Advantages of AI in Dance Creation**

1. **Enhanced Creativity:** AI can suggest new dance moves, rhythms, and styles, offering choreographers a fresh perspective.
2. **Time Efficiency:** AI can help automate the process of creating and refining dance pieces, saving time and effort.
3. **Cultural Integration:** AI can analyze and integrate various cultural elements, creating dance pieces that reflect a fusion of different cultures.

#### 1.1.3 The Importance of Cultural Integration

Cultural integration in dance is vital for several reasons:

1. **Promotion of Cultural Diversity:** By integrating various cultural elements, dance can become a platform for celebrating diversity.
2. **Breaking Down Barriers:** Dance that incorporates different cultural elements can help to break down cultural barriers and promote understanding.
3. **Innovation and Creativity:** Cultural integration often leads to innovative and creative dance pieces that push the boundaries of traditional dance forms.
