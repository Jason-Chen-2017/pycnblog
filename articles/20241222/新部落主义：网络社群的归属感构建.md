#### 1.1 Background and Significance of Online Communities

**1.1.1 The Rise of Online Communities**

In the late 20th century, the advent of the internet revolutionized human communication, leading to the creation of online communities. These digital spaces provided platforms for individuals to connect, share interests, and collaborate regardless of geographical boundaries. Early online communities such as Usenet and Bulletin Board Systems (BBS) laid the groundwork for what we now know as social media platforms.

As the 21st century progressed, social media platforms like Facebook, Twitter, Reddit, and more recently, Twitch and Discord, have become integral parts of our daily lives. These platforms have not only changed how we interact with each other but have also reshaped social structures and dynamics. The rise of mobile devices and increased internet penetration have further fueled the growth of online communities, making them accessible to a broader audience.

**1.1.2 The Need for a Sense of Belonging**

The digital age has brought numerous benefits, but it has also led to feelings of isolation and disconnection. While online communities provide a means of connecting with others, they can also contribute to a sense of loneliness and alienation. The anonymity and distance inherent in online interactions can make it challenging to form deep, meaningful connections.

The need for a sense of belonging in online communities is driven by several factors. Firstly, humans are inherently social beings, and the desire to belong is a fundamental human need. Secondly, online communities offer a sense of identity and purpose, which can be particularly appealing to individuals who feel disconnected in their offline lives. Finally, a strong sense of belonging can lead to increased engagement and loyalty within a community, benefiting both the individual members and the community as a whole.

**1.1.3 The Concept of New Tribes Movement**

The concept of New Tribes Movement (NTM) originated from the need to understand and leverage the dynamics of online communities in a way that fosters a sense of belonging. NTM is not just a movement but a framework that encompasses a set of principles and strategies aimed at creating thriving, engaged, and cohesive online communities.

NTM is rooted in social psychology theories of group formation and identity, emphasizing the importance of shared values, mutual support, and a sense of purpose. It focuses on building community structures that promote inclusivity, respect, and collaboration. NTM also recognizes the role of technology in facilitating community interaction and engagement, leveraging digital tools and platforms to create meaningful connections.
