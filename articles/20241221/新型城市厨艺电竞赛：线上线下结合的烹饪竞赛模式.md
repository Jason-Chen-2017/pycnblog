Before diving into the detailed structure of the book, it's essential to clarify the core concepts and terms that will be used throughout the content. This will provide a solid foundation for readers to grasp the concepts easily.

**Core Concepts:**

- **Urban Culinary Competition:** A cooking contest that combines online and offline elements, typically held in urban settings.
- **Online Cooking Contest:** A cooking competition where participants submit their dishes through digital platforms, often using video submissions.
- **Offline Culinary Event:** A traditional cooking contest where participants compete in real-time at a physical venue.
- **Blended Model:** The integration of online and offline components to create a hybrid competition format.
