#### Chapter 1: Introduction to Postmodernism and the Deconstruction of Meaning

The term "postmodernism" refers to a range of intellectual and cultural movements that emerged in the mid-20th century, as a response to modernism. While modernism celebrated reason, progress, and the unity of art forms, postmodernism questioned these notions, advocating for diversity, multiplicity, and the fragmentation of meaning. This chapter will delve into the origins and development of postmodernism, focusing on how it challenges traditional notions of meaning and truth.
