### Step 3: Fundamental Concepts of Prompt Engineering

#### Core Principles of Prompt Engineering:
1. **Clarity**: A well-designed prompt should be clear and easy to understand.
2. **Relevance**: The prompt should be relevant to the task at hand.
3. **Completeness**: It should provide all necessary information for the task.
4. **Bias-Free**: Avoid cultural or language biases that could lead to misunderstandings.

#### Types of Prompts and Their Characteristics:
- **Open-Ended Prompts**: Allow for free-form responses and are useful for generating creative or diverse answers.
- **Closed-Ended Prompts**: Limit responses to a predefined set of options and are often used for factual questions.
- **Scenario-Based Prompts**: Present a scenario and ask for a response, which is useful for storytelling or role-playing tasks.

#### The Role of Language in Prompt Engineering:
