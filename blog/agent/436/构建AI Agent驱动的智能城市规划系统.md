#### Core Concepts

**AI Agents**

AI agents are autonomous entities that can perceive their environment through sensors, take actions based on their understanding, and interact with the environment to achieve specific goals. In the context of urban planning, AI agents are designed to perform tasks such as traffic management, environmental monitoring, resource allocation, and urban simulation. These agents operate within a defined environment, which can be a physical city or a virtual model of the urban landscape.

**Machine Learning**

Machine Learning (ML) is a subset of artificial intelligence that focuses on the development of algorithms that can learn from and make predictions or decisions based on data. In urban planning, ML algorithms are used to analyze large datasets, identify patterns, and make data-driven decisions. Common ML techniques used in urban planning include regression analysis, clustering, and classification.

**Data Analytics**

Data Analytics is the science of examining raw data with the purpose of drawing conclusions about that information. In urban planning, data analytics is crucial for understanding the current state of the city, predicting future trends, and evaluating the impact of planning decisions. Data analytics tools and techniques include data mining, statistical analysis, and predictive modeling.

**Urban Simulation**

Urban Simulation involves creating models of urban environments that can be used to simulate and predict the behavior of cities under various scenarios. These simulations help urban planners understand the impacts of different planning decisions on traffic flow, pollution levels, population distribution, and other urban metrics.

#### Terminology

**Agent-Based Modeling (ABM)**

Agent-Based Modeling is a computational technique used to study the behavior of complex systems by modeling the interactions of multiple autonomous agents. In urban planning, ABM is used to simulate the behavior of individual agents (such as people, vehicles, or businesses) and their collective impact on the urban environment.

**Rule-Based Systems**

Rule-Based Systems are a type of AI system that makes decisions based on a set of predefined rules. In urban planning, rule-based systems can be used to create algorithms that automate certain planning tasks, such as zoning regulations or traffic signal control.

**Deep Learning**

Deep Learning is a subfield of machine learning that focuses on algorithms inspired by the structure and function of the human brain. Deep learning models, such as neural networks, are particularly effective at processing and analyzing large volumes of data, making them well-suited for urban planning tasks that require complex pattern recognition.

**Optimization Algorithms**

Optimization Algorithms are used to find the best possible solution to a problem within a defined set of constraints. In urban planning, optimization algorithms can be used to optimize resource allocation, transportation routes, or building layouts to achieve specific objectives, such as minimizing pollution or maximizing accessibility.

**Blockchain**

Blockchain is a decentralized digital ledger technology that can be used to securely record and verify transactions. In urban planning, blockchain can be used to create transparent and tamper-proof records of land ownership, property transactions, and other urban data.
