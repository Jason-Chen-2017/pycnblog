- **Background Introduction:**
  - Linear programming is a mathematical method used to find the optimal allocation of limited resources to maximize profit or minimize cost.

- **Problem Description:**
  - In crop planning, farmers need to determine the optimal amount of each crop to plant, taking into account constraints such as available land, water, and labor.

- **Solution and Discussion:**

  - For example, a linear programming model can help farmers determine the most profitable crop mix based on market prices and resource availability.

- **Boundary and Extension:**
