- **Environmental Conditions**: Climate, natural resources, and pollution levels can impact where people choose to live.

##### 2.2.3 Population Distribution Models

To understand and predict population distribution, researchers use various models:

- **Gravity Model**: A model that predicts the migration of individuals based on the economic strength of two regions and the distance between them.
- **Push and Pull Model**: This model explains population movement by considering factors that push people away from their current location (e.g., poverty, unemployment) and factors that attract them to a new location (e.g., job opportunities, better living conditions).
- **Space Syntax Model**: This model analyzes the spatial structure of urban areas to understand how they influence population distribution and movement.
