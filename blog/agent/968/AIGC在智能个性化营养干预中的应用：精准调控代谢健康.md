**3.3 Mathematical Models and Formulas**

**3.3.1 Neural Network Model Formulas**

The neural network model involves a series of linear transformations and activation functions. The general formula for a neural network is:

$$
y = \sigma(W \cdot x + b)
$$

where \( y \) is the output, \( \sigma \) is the activation function, \( W \) is the weight matrix, \( x \) is the input vector, and \( b \) is the bias vector.

**3.3.2 Optimization Algorithm Formulas**

Gradient Descent is a common optimization algorithm used in training neural networks. The update rule for Gradient Descent is:

$$
\theta = \theta - \alpha \cdot \nabla_\theta J(\theta)
$$

where \( \theta \) is the parameter vector, \( \alpha \) is the learning rate, and \( J(\theta) \) is the loss function.

**3.3.3 Adaptive Learning Rate Formulas**

Adaptive learning rates, such as AdaGrad and RMSprop, adjust the learning rate based on the historical gradients. The AdaGrad update rule is:

$$
\theta = \theta - \frac{\alpha}{\sqrt{\sum_{t=0}^{T}(g_t)^2}} \cdot g_t
$$

where \( g_t \) is the gradient at time \( t \).
