**Types of AI Agents**:

AI agents can be broadly categorized into several types based on their functionalities:

1. **Expert Systems**: These agents are designed to emulate the decision-making ability of a human expert in a specific domain. They use a set of predefined rules and knowledge bases to provide solutions to complex problems.

2. **Reactive Machines**: These agents react to specific inputs without any historical context. They do not learn or adapt over time but can be highly efficient in certain scenarios.

3. **Model-Based Reflex Agents**: These agents use a model of the environment to make decisions based on current and predicted states. They can adapt their behavior based on past experiences.

4. **Theory-Based Reflex Agents**: Similar to model-based reflex agents, but they use theories about the world to make decisions, which can lead to more complex and adaptive behavior.

5. **Goal-Based Agents**: These agents operate with specific goals in mind and continuously adapt their behavior to achieve these goals, even in changing environments.

6. **Utility-Based Agents**: These agents make decisions based on maximizing utility or value, often using probabilistic models and utility functions.

**Functionalities**:

The functionalities of AI agents are vast and depend on their specific design and application. Some of the key functionalities include:

1. **Data Collection**: AI agents can collect data from various sources such as sensors, cameras, and IoT devices.

2. **Data Analysis**: Once data is collected, AI agents can analyze it to identify patterns, anomalies, and trends.

3. **Prediction and Forecasting**: Using historical data and machine learning algorithms, AI agents can predict future events or conditions.

4. **Decision-Making**: AI agents can make autonomous decisions based on collected data and predefined rules or learned patterns.

5. **Task Automation**: AI agents can automate repetitive tasks, freeing up human resources for more complex or high-value activities.

6. **Resource Allocation**: AI agents can optimize resource allocation by predicting demand and managing resources efficiently.

7. **Communication and Coordination**: AI agents can communicate with other agents or systems to coordinate tasks and share information.

**Summary**:

In summary, AI agents are intelligent entities designed to perform specific tasks autonomously. They come in various types, each with its own set of functionalities.
