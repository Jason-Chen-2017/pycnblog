#### 1.1 Problem Background

**1.1.1 Global Environmental Pollution Overview**

Environmental pollution is a pressing global issue that affects air, water, and soil quality. Key pollutants include particulate matter (PM2.5 and PM10), nitrogen oxides (NOx), sulfur oxides (SOx), volatile organic compounds (VOCs), and heavy metals. These pollutants originate from various sources, such as industrial activities, transportation, agricultural practices, and waste management. The World Health Organization (WHO) reports that air pollution alone contributes to millions of premature deaths annually, highlighting the severity of the issue.

**1.1.2 The Urgency of Environmental Pollution Control**

Environmental pollution has significant consequences for human health, biodiversity, and climate change. Air and water pollution can lead to respiratory and cardiovascular diseases, neurological disorders, and cancer. Moreover, pollution disrupts ecosystems, leading to the decline of fish populations, loss of vegetation, and extinction of species. Climate change exacerbated by greenhouse gas emissions further compounds these problems, resulting in more frequent natural disasters, rising sea levels, and extreme weather events. Addressing environmental pollution is therefore crucial for ensuring a sustainable future.

**1.1.3 Challenges in Current Pollution Monitoring and Sourcing**

Current pollution monitoring and sourcing methods face several challenges. Traditional methods often rely on point-source monitoring, which is limited in scope and cannot capture the complex interactions and transport processes of pollutants. Additionally, these methods typically suffer from high costs, time consumption, and insufficient spatial coverage. Another challenge is the difficulty in distinguishing between multiple pollution sources, especially in densely populated and industrialized areas. Furthermore, the integration of diverse data sources, such as satellite imagery, ground-level sensors, and atmospheric models, is often complex and inefficient.

#### 1.2 Concept Definition and Overview

**1.2.1 Definition of Environmental Pollution**

Environmental pollution refers to the introduction of harmful substances or energy into the environment, causing adverse effects on human health, ecosystems, and climate. These substances can be gases, liquids, or solids and originate from both natural and human activities.

**1.2.2 Concepts Related to Pollution Sourcing and Analysis**

 Pollution sourcing involves identifying and attributing the origin of pollutants to specific sources. Key concepts include emission inventories, pollution plume modeling, and source apportionment techniques.

Innovative methods like Mind Chain are essential for improving the accuracy and efficiency of pollution sourcing.
