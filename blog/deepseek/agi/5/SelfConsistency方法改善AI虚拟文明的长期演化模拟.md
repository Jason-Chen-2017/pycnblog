7. **Evaluate Adjustments**: Assess the effectiveness of the applied adjustments by monitoring the behavior of the agents over time. If new discrepancies or inconsistencies arise, repeat steps 4-7 to further refine the agents' behavior.

8. **Repeat**: Continue the process in a loop, continually monitoring, analyzing, and adjusting agent behavior to maintain the self-consistency of the simulation.
