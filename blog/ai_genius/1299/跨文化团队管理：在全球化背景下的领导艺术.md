### Step 2: Core Concepts of Cross-Cultural Teams

**Definition of Culture**
"Culture is a complex system of shared beliefs, values, behaviors, and symbols that shape the way individuals within a group think, feel, and act. It encompasses both visible aspects, such as language and customs, and invisible dimensions, including attitudes and worldviews. Understanding the nuances of culture is essential for effective cross-cultural team management. Different cultures prioritize different values, which can affect how team members perceive and respond to various situations."

**Dimensions of Cultural Differences**
"There are several dimensions through which cultural differences can be analyzed, each impacting team dynamics in unique ways. Key dimensions include:
- **Individualism vs. Collectivism**: Individualistic cultures emphasize personal achievements and independence, while collectivist cultures prioritize group cohesion and interdependence.
- **Power Distance**: Cultures with high power distance tend to have significant hierarchical structures, while those with low power distance are more egalitarian.
- **Uncertainty Avoidance**: Cultures that have a high tolerance for uncertainty are more flexible and adaptable, while those with low tolerance for uncertainty prefer structured and predictable environments.
- **Masculinity vs. Femininity**: Masculine cultures focus on competition, achievement, and material success, while feminine cultures emphasize cooperation, quality of life, and nurturance."
**Cultural Adaptation Strategies**
"To thrive in a cross-cultural environment, individuals and organizations must adopt strategies that facilitate cultural adaptation. These strategies include:
- **Cognitive Adjustment**: Recognizing and understanding cultural differences to reframe situations from a new perspective.
- **Behavioral Adjustment**: Modifying behaviors to be more in line with the cultural norms of the new environment.

        return "Ohayou!"

        return 48
    elif culture == "arabic":
        return 45
