```python
psi = schroedinger_wavefunction(np.linspace(0, L, 1000))

# 绘制波函数
plt.plot(x, np.abs(psi)**2)
plt.xlabel('Position (x)')
plt.ylabel('Probability Density')
plt.title('Quantum Tunneling Wavefunction')
plt.show()
```
