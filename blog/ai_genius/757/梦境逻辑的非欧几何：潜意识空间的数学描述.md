Through an iterative process of feedback and refinement, the generator improves its ability to produce realistic dream sequences.

**Pseudocode:**

```plaintext
Function DreamEngine(DreamDataset)

    Dreams = LoadDreamDataset(DreamDataset)

    Generator = TrainGenerator(Dreams)
    Discriminator = TrainDiscriminator(Dreams)
    
    // Iterate through training process
    For i = 1 to Iterations
        GeneratedDreams = GenerateDreams(Generator)
        DiscriminatorScore = EvaluateDiscriminator(GeneratedDreams, Discriminator)
        
        // Update generator and discriminator based on feedback

        Discriminator = UpdateDiscriminator(Generator, Dreams)
    End For
    
    Return Generator
```
