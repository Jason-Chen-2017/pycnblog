## Introduction

### 1.1 Concept and Background of Neural Turing Machine

#### 1.1.1 Introduction to Neural Turing Machine and Its Development

The concept of the Neural Turing Machine (NTM) was first proposed by Alex Graves et al. in 2014. NTMs are a type of neural network that combines the flexibility of neural networks with the computational power of Turing machines. This hybrid architecture enables NTMs to handle complex tasks that traditional neural networks struggle with.

#### 1.1.2 Comparison between Neural Turing Machines and Traditional Turing Machines

- **Traditional Turing Machines**: Based on logic and algorithms, with fixed operational sequences, not adaptable to dynamic environments.
- **Neural Turing Machines**: Combining the flexibility of neural networks, suitable for dynamic and complex environments.
