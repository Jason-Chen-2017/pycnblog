$$

2. **变分自编码器损失函数**：

$$
L_VAE = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\Bigg[\frac{1}{2}\sum_{j=1}^{K}\log(p(x_i|z_i^{(j)})) + \frac{1}{2}\sum_{j=1}^{K}\log(p(z_i^{(j)})) - x_i\cdot \log(\hat{x}_i)\Bigg]
