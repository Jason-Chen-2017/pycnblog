##### 3.1.2 解码器的原理

```python
def decoder(hidden):
    W_decode = np.random.randn(hidden_dim, output_dim)
    b_decode = np.zeros(output_dim)
    output = np.dot(hidden, W_decode) + b_decode
    return output
```

#### 提示词生成的概率公式  
$$ P(\text{提示词} | \text{输入}) = \prod_{i=1}^{n} p(w_i | w_{<i}) $$  

#### 损失函数  
$$ J = -\sum_{i=1}^{n} \log p(w_i | w_{<i}) $$

$$ \Delta W = \frac{\partial J}{\partial W} $$
