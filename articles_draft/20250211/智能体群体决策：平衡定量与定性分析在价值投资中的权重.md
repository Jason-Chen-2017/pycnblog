- 3.3.1 定量分析的数学模型
  $$ V = \sum_{i=1}^{n} w_i x_i $$
- 3.3.2 定性分析的数学模型
  $$ Q = \sum_{j=1}^{m} w_j y_j $$
- 3.3.3 权重平衡公式
  $$ w_i = \frac{V_i}{\sum_{k=1}^{n} V_k} $$

### 3.4 Python代码实现
```python
def calculate_weights(values):
    total = sum(values)
    weights = [v / total for v in values]
    return weights

quantitative_values = [0.4, 0.3, 0.2]
qualitative_values = [0.15, 0.25, 0.1]

quantitative_weights = calculate_weights(quantitative_values)
qualitative_weights = calculate_weights(qualitative_values)

print("定量分析权重:", quantitative_weights)
print("定性分析权重:", qualitative_weights)
```
