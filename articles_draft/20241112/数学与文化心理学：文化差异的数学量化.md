**Chapter 1: Overview of Mathematics and Cultural Psychology**

1. **1.1 Definition and Scope of Mathematics and Cultural Psychology**

   **Background Introduction:**
   Mathematics and cultural psychology are two diverse fields of study that, when combined, offer a unique lens through which to examine the nuances of human behavior and cognition. Mathematics is the science of numbers, shapes, and patterns, often seen as a universal language due to its applicability across various disciplines. Cultural psychology, on the other hand, explores how culture shapes the way individuals perceive, think, feel, and behave.
