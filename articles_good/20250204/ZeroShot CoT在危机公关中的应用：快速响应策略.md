### Chapter 1: Introduction to Zero-Shot CoT and Crisis Communication

#### 1.1 Conceptual Background

**Definition of Zero-Shot CoT**

Zero-Shot CoT, or Zero-Shot Coreference Resolution, is a natural language processing (NLP) technique that enables the identification and resolution of pronouns and other referring expressions without requiring any prior training data on the specific entities involved. Unlike traditional coreference resolution methods that rely on a large corpus of annotated data to learn the relationships between entities, Zero-Shot CoT leverages generalizable patterns and semantics to infer coreference links even when the involved entities are unseen during training.

**The Role of Zero-Shot CoT in Crisis Communication**

In the realm of Crisis Communication, Zero-Shot CoT plays a crucial role by enhancing the ability to understand and interpret the context of rapidly evolving situations. During a crisis, there is often a surge of information, including press releases, social media updates, and public statements. Zero-Shot CoT can help in automatically disambiguating references and pronouns, ensuring consistent and coherent communication across various platforms and sources.
