        self.size = size
        self.plants = []

    def add_plant(self, plant):
        self.plants.append(plant)

    def calculate_weight(self):
        weight = 0
        for plant in self.plants:
            weight += plant.height * self.size
        return weight

class Plant:
    def __init__(self, species, height, water_requirements):

### Chapter 5: Construction and Maintenance of Urban Roof Gardens

#### 5.1 Construction Process

The construction of an urban roof garden involves several critical steps to ensure its success and longevity. Below is a detailed breakdown of the construction process:

**1. Initial Planning and Design Approval:**
- **Site Assessment:** Conduct a comprehensive site assessment to determine the rooftop's structural integrity, orientation, and potential challenges.
- **Design Approval:** Obtain necessary permits and approvals from local authorities. Ensure the design meets safety standards and environmental regulations.
- **Budget and Timeline:** Establish a budget and timeline for the project, considering materials, labor, and equipment.

**2. Structural Preparation:**
- **Roof Inspection:** Inspect the roof for any damage or defects that may need repair before starting the garden construction.
- **Load Testing:** If necessary, perform load tests to confirm the roof's capacity to support the garden's weight.
- **Waterproofing:** Apply a waterproof membrane to protect the roof from water damage caused by the garden.

**3. Garden Foundation:**
- **Base Layer:** Install a lightweight, well-draining base layer made of organic materials such as coconut coir or compost.
- **Patching and Leveling:** Patch any uneven areas on the roof and ensure the garden area is level.

**4. Planting:**
- **Seedling Preparation:** Start seedlings in a greenhouse or nursery to ensure they are healthy and ready for transplanting.
- **Planting Design:** Follow the garden design plan to plant a variety of plants suitable for the rooftop environment.
- **Soil Preparation:** Mix the growing medium with organic matter to provide nutrients and improve soil structure.

**5. Irrigation and Drainage System:**
- **Irrigation Setup:** Install an automated irrigation system with drip emitters or sprinklers to ensure even water distribution.
- **Drainage System:** Create a drainage system to manage excess water and prevent waterlogging.

**6. Weatherproofing and Protective Measures:**
- **Wind Protection:** Use windbreaks or structures to protect plants from strong winds.
- **Temperature Control:** If necessary, install shading devices or insulation to protect plants from extreme temperatures.

**7. Final Touches:**
- **Pathways and Seating:** Install pathways and seating areas to enhance the garden's usability.
- **Safety Equipment:** Install safety equipment such as railing and non-slip surfaces.

**8. Post-Construction Maintenance:**
- **Regular Inspections:** Conduct regular inspections to check for signs of wear and tear, leaks, or other issues.
- **Pruning and Weeding:** Maintain plants by pruning, watering, and removing weeds.
- **Winterization:** Prepare the garden for winter by covering plants, insulating pipes, and reducing water use.

#### 5.2 Key Technologies in Construction

**1. Green Roof Systems:**
- **Living Roof:** This type of green roof is composed of a growing medium and plants. It provides insulation, reduces the urban heat island effect, and absorbs rainwater.
- **Extensive Roof:** Designed for low-maintenance plants, such as sedum, it's lightweight and easy to install.
- **Intensive Roof:** Can support a wider variety of plants and requires more maintenance. It's heavier and more complex to install.

**2. Waterproofing Membranes:**
- **Bitumen Membranes:** Commonly used for their durability and flexibility.
- **Polymer-modified Bitumen:** Offers improved resistance to aging and extreme temperatures.
- **Polyvinyl Chloride (PVC):** Known for its chemical resistance and longevity.

**3. Irrigation Systems:**
- **Drip Irrigation:** Efficiently delivers water directly to the roots of plants, minimizing water waste.
- **Sprinkler Systems:** Sprays water over a wide area, simulating natural rainfall.
- **Smart Irrigation:** Uses sensors and automated controls to adjust watering based on weather conditions and plant needs.

**4. Drainage Systems:**
- **Subdrainage:** Designed to remove excess water from the growing medium and prevent waterlogging.
- **Surface Drainage:** Allows water to flow off the roof surface through gutters and downspouts.

#### 5.3 Maintenance Management

**1. Regular Inspections:**
- Inspect the roof garden periodically for any signs of damage, such as leaks, cracks, or plant disease.
- Check the structural integrity of the rooftop, especially after severe weather events.

**2. Pruning and Weeding:**
- Regularly prune plants to maintain their health and shape.
- Remove weeds to prevent competition for resources and to reduce the risk of pests and diseases.

**3. Irrigation System Maintenance:**
- Check and clean irrigation heads to ensure they are functioning properly.
- Inspect the irrigation pipes and connections for leaks or damage.

**4. Temperature and Humidity Control:**
- Monitor the temperature and humidity levels in the garden to ensure they are within the optimal range for plant growth.
- Take appropriate measures to protect plants during extreme weather conditions.

**5. Pest and Disease Control:**
- Identify and treat any pest or disease problems promptly to prevent them from spreading.
- Use organic pest control methods whenever possible to minimize environmental impact.

**6. Winterization:**
- Prepare the garden for winter by covering plants, insulating pipes, and reducing water use.
- Remove any debris and ensure all systems are in good working order before the cold weather sets in.
