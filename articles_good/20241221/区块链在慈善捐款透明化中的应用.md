#### 1.1 Blockchain Basics

**Concept Definition:**
Blockchain is a decentralized and distributed ledger technology that allows the secure recording of transactions across many computers. It is maintained through consensus mechanisms, which ensure the integrity and authenticity of the data.

**Historical Background:**
Blockchain technology was introduced by an anonymous person or group under the pseudonym Satoshi Nakamoto in 2008. The first blockchain implementation was the Bitcoin network, which went live in 2009. Since then, blockchain has evolved and expanded its applications beyond cryptocurrencies.

**Core Technologies and Characteristics:**
- **Decentralization:** Blockchain operates independently of a central authority, distributing the control across a network of participants.
- **Immutability:** Once data is recorded on the blockchain, it cannot be altered or deleted, ensuring data integrity.
- **Transparency:** All transactions are recorded publicly on the blockchain, allowing for easy auditing and verification.
- **Security:** Blockchain employs cryptographic techniques to secure transactions and prevent fraud.

**Blockchain Ecosystem and Application Fields:**
Blockchain technology has found applications in various fields beyond cryptocurrencies. These include supply chain management, healthcare, voting systems, and more. Each application utilizes the unique characteristics of blockchain to enhance security, transparency, and efficiency.

### Step 3: Core Concepts and Mechanisms

#### 3.1 Blockchain Architecture and Operation

**Blockchain Architecture:**
Blockchain consists of multiple blocks, each containing a list of transactions. These blocks are linked together in a chain, with each block referencing the previous one through a cryptographic hash.

**Operation Principles:**
- **Transaction:** A transaction is a record of value transfer between two parties.
- **Block:** A block is a container for a group of transactions.
- **Mining:** Miners validate transactions and add them to the blockchain, securing the network and earning rewards.

**Consensus Mechanisms:**
Blockchain networks use consensus mechanisms to agree on the validity of transactions and the order of blocks. Common consensus mechanisms include Proof of Work (PoW) and Proof of Stake (PoS).

区块链技术是一种分布式账本技术，通过加密和共识机制确保数据的不可篡改和透明性。自2008年比特币的诞生以来，区块链技术迅速发展，并在金融、物流、医疗等多个领域得到了广泛应用。近年来，区块链技术开始被引入到慈善捐款领域，以解决传统慈善捐款过程中存在的信息不对称、透明度不足等问题。

### 3.1 区块链的基本架构与运作原理

区块链的基本架构包括数据层、网络层、共识层和应用层。数据层包括区块和链结构，网络层包括P2P网络和共识算法，共识层确保数据的共识和一致性，应用层包括智能合约和去中心化应用。

区块链的运作原理如下：

1. **交易提出**：用户发起交易请求，交易包括发送者和接收者的信息以及交易金额。
2. **交易验证**：网络中的节点对交易进行验证，确保交易的有效性和合法性。
3. **区块创建**：验证通过的交易被纳入一个新的区块中。
4. **区块验证**：网络中的节点对新区块进行验证，确保其合法性和完整性。
5. **区块添加**：验证通过的区块被添加到区块链上，成为永久记录。

contract DonorChoose {
    address public owner;
    mapping(address => uint256) public donations;

    constructor() {
        owner = msg.sender;
    }

        require(msg.value > 0, "捐赠金额必须大于0");
        donations[msg.sender] += msg.value;
    }
