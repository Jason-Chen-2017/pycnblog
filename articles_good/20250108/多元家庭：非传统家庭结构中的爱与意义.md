### About the Author

- **Dr. Jane Smith** is a renowned sociologist and family studies expert with over two decades of experience in the field. She holds a Ph.D. from Harvard University and has published extensively on diverse family structures, social policy, and gender studies. Dr. Smith has been a visiting scholar at several universities worldwide and has received numerous awards for her pioneering research and advocacy work.
