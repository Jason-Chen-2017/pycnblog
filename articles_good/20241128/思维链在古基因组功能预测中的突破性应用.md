**5.2.2 Mathematical Models and Formulas**

1. **Dynamic Bayesian Network (DBN)**:
   - The DBN is a probabilistic graphical model that represents a hierarchy of variables using conditional probability distributions. The model is defined as follows:
     $$ P(x_1, x_2, ..., x_n) = \prod_{t=1}^{n} P(x_t | x_{t-1}, ..., x_1) $$
   - The parameters of the DBN are learned using maximum likelihood estimation (MLE) or Bayesian inference.

2. **Recurrent Neural Network (RNN)**:
   - An RNN is a class of neural networks that can process sequential data. The model is defined as follows:
     $$ h_t = \sigma(W_h h_{t-1} + W_x x_t + b_h) $$
     $$ y_t = \sigma(W_y h_t + b_y) $$
   - Where \( h_t \) is the hidden state at time \( t \), \( x_t \) is the input at time \( t \), \( y_t \) is the output at time \( t \), \( \sigma \) is the activation function (typically a sigmoid or tanh function), and \( W_h \), \( W_x \), \( W_y \), and \( b_h \), \( b_y \) are the model parameters.

3. **Convolutional Neural Network (CNN)**:
   - A CNN is a class of neural networks that can efficiently process grid-like data structures, such as images or genomic sequences. The model is defined as follows:
     $$ h_t = \text{ReLU}(W_h \odot h_{t-1} + W_x \odot x_t + b_h) $$
     $$ y_t = \text{ReLU}(W_y \odot h_t + b_y) $$
   - Where \( \odot \) denotes the convolution operation, and \( \text{ReLU} \) is the Rectified Linear Unit activation function.
