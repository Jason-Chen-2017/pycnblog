    tones = changes.count('全音')
    compound_changes = total_changes - semi_tones - tones
    probability_semi_tone = semi_tones / total_changes
    probability_tone = tones / total_changes
    probability_compound = compound_changes / total_changes
    return probability_semi_tone, probability_tone, probability_compound

在音乐制作过程中，数学方法可以帮助音乐制作人精确控制音乐的节奏、音高和动态变化。以下是一些常见的数学方法应用实例：

**示例**：

$$

$$
\text{弦3}: f_3 = 440 \text{ Hz} \times 2^{-3/12}
