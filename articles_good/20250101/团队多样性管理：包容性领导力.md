#### 3.3 Differences from Traditional Leadership

### 5.1 Theories of Diversity

#### 5.1.1 Types of Diversity

Diversity encompasses a wide range of characteristics, including race, ethnicity, gender, age, sexual orientation, physical abilities, and cultural background. Understanding the different types of diversity is essential for developing effective diversity management strategies.

**Table 1: Types of Diversity**

| Type        | Description                                                                                   |
|-------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Race        | Genetic characteristics that define human populations.                                            |
| Ethnicity   | Shared cultural characteristics, such as language, traditions, and customs.                         |
| Gender      | The societal roles and expectations associated with being male or female.                         |
| Age         | Differences in age among team members.                                                           |
| Sexual Orientation | Personal identification and attraction, such as heterosexual, homosexual, bisexual, or asexual. |
| Physical Abilities | Differences in physical capabilities, such as mobility, vision, or hearing.                      |
| Cultural Background | Shared beliefs, values, and practices that shape individual and group behavior.                  |
