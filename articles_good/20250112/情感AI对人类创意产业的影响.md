#### 1.1 Background and Definition of Emotional AI

Emotional AI, or affective computing, is a branch of artificial intelligence that focuses on enabling machines to understand, interpret, process, and simulate human emotions. This field emerged from the need to create more intuitive and natural interactions between humans and machines. Unlike traditional AI, which primarily deals with logical reasoning and data processing, Emotional AI seeks to understand the emotional context behind human behaviors and expressions.

The key concepts in Emotional AI include:

- **Emotion Recognition**: The process of identifying and classifying human emotions from various sources like voice, facial expressions, and physiological signals.
- **Emotion Generation**: Creating realistic emotional responses in machines to mimic human-like interactions.
- **Emotion Regulation**: Modifying the emotional state of machines to better suit the needs of the interaction.

**Emergence of Emotional AI**

The development of Emotional AI can be traced back to the early 1990s when computer scientists and psychologists started exploring the idea of creating machines that could understand and respond to human emotions. The advent of advanced algorithms, machine learning techniques, and access to vast amounts of data have accelerated the progress in this field. Today, Emotional AI is not just a theoretical concept but has practical applications in various industries, including healthcare, customer service, and creative industries.
