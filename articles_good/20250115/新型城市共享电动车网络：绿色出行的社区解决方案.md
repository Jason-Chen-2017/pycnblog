#### 2.1.1 Definition of Shared Electric Bicycle Systems

A shared electric bicycle system is a network of electric bicycles that are available for shared use by the public. These systems are typically operated by private companies or public agencies and can be accessed through mobile applications or kiosks. The primary goal of shared electric bicycle systems is to provide a flexible and affordable transportation option for short-distance travel within urban areas.

Shared electric bicycle systems are characterized by the following key features:

- **Mobility-as-a-Service (MaaS):** These systems offer mobility on demand, allowing users to access electric bicycles whenever and wherever they need them.
- **Dockless Operation:** Unlike traditional bike-sharing systems that require users to return the bicycles to designated docking stations, shared electric bicycles can be parked anywhere within the service area.
- **Electric Power:** Electric bicycles are powered by rechargeable batteries, providing an eco-friendly alternative to traditional gasoline-powered vehicles.
- **Digital Integration:** Shared electric bicycle systems are typically supported by mobile applications that allow users to locate, unlock, and pay for bicycles.

#### 2.1.2 Components of Shared Electric Bicycle Systems

Shared electric bicycle systems comprise several key components, each playing a crucial role in their operation and success. These components include:

1. **Electric Bicycles:** The electric bicycles are the core of the shared electric bicycle system. They are typically lightweight, easy to maneuver, and equipped with a rechargeable battery. Some models also feature a throttle that allows users to power the bicycle without pedaling.

2. **Mobile Application:** The mobile application serves as the user interface for the shared electric bicycle system. It allows users to locate nearby bicycles, unlock them using a digital key, and make payments. The application also provides real-time information about the availability and status of bicycles within the network.

3. **Sensors and Tracking Devices:** Shared electric bicycles are equipped with various sensors and tracking devices that monitor their usage, location, and battery status. These devices are essential for the efficient operation of the system, enabling real-time data collection and analysis.

4. **Central Management System:** The central management system is the backbone of the shared electric bicycle network. It is responsible for monitoring the performance of the system, managing user accounts, scheduling maintenance, and ensuring the availability of bicycles and charging stations.

5. **Charging Stations:** Charging stations are essential for recharging the batteries of shared electric bicycles. They can be located throughout the service area, ensuring that bicycles are always available for users.

6. **Payment and Billing System:** The payment and billing system enables users to pay for their rides using various payment methods, such as credit cards, mobile wallets, or contactless payment options.

#### 2.1.3 Operational Workflow of Shared Electric Bicycle Systems

The operational workflow of shared electric bicycle systems can be summarized in the following steps:

1. **User Registration:** Users download the mobile application and register for an account. They can then access the app's features, such as locating nearby bicycles and viewing route information.

2. **Bicycle Location and Unlocking:** Using the mobile application, users locate an available electric bicycle within the service area. They use the app to unlock the bicycle by scanning a QR code or entering a digital key.

3. **Ride and Payment:** Users ride the electric bicycle to their destination and lock it at the end of their journey. They use the mobile application to make a payment for the ride, which is typically based on time or distance traveled.

4. **Data Collection and Analysis:** The central management system collects data from sensors and tracking devices on each bicycle, including usage patterns, battery status, and maintenance needs. This data is analyzed to optimize the system's performance and ensure the availability of bicycles and charging stations.

5. **Maintenance and Management:** The central management system schedules maintenance for bicycles and charging stations based on their usage patterns and battery status. This helps ensure that the system remains reliable and efficient.

6. **Feedback and Improvement:** Users provide feedback on their experience with the shared electric bicycle system, which is used to improve the system's performance and user experience.

The Sevici program has been highly successful, with the number of users increasing significantly each year. The program has contributed to a reduction in traffic congestion and air pollution, and it has received positive reviews from users for its convenience and reliability.

### 2.6 Challenges and Countermeasures in the Implementation of New Urban Shared Electric Bicycle Networks

The implementation of new urban shared electric bicycle networks involves numerous challenges that must be addressed to ensure their success and sustainability. This section will discuss the main challenges encountered during the implementation process and propose corresponding countermeasures to overcome these obstacles.

#### 2.6.1 Infrastructure Challenges

**Problem:** Insufficient infrastructure, including inadequate bike lanes, parking facilities, and charging stations, can hinder the effective operation of shared electric bicycle networks.

**Problem:** Lack of specific regulations for shared electric bicycle networks can lead to safety concerns, legal issues, and user dissatisfaction.

#### 2.6.3 User Education Challenges

**Problem:** Users may lack knowledge about safe and responsible usage of shared electric bicycles, leading to accidents and misuse of the network.

**Countermeasure:** Public awareness campaigns and educational programs should be conducted to educate users about safe cycling practices, the rules of the road, and the importance of responsible usage. User-friendly guides and instructions should be provided at bike stations and through mobile applications.

#### 2.6.4 User Experience Challenges

**Problem:** Issues such as bike availability, user-friendly interfaces, and payment systems can negatively impact user satisfaction and adoption of shared electric bicycle networks.

**Countermeasure:** Continuous user feedback and analysis should be incorporated into the design and improvement of shared electric bicycle networks. Mobile applications should be intuitive and user-friendly, ensuring a seamless user experience. Efforts should also be made to ensure bike availability and reduce waiting times for users.

#### 2.6.5 Data Management Challenges

**Problem:** Efficient data collection, storage, and analysis are critical for the optimization of shared electric bicycle networks but can be challenging due to the large volume of data generated.

**Countermeasure:** Implementing advanced data analytics tools and technologies, such as machine learning and artificial intelligence, can help in processing and analyzing large volumes of data. Data management systems should be designed to ensure data security, privacy, and compliance with relevant regulations.

#### 2.6.6 Integration Challenges

**Problem:** Integrating shared electric bicycle networks with existing public transit systems can be complex and require careful planning.

**Countermeasure:** Collaborative efforts between public transit authorities and shared electric bicycle operators are essential for successful integration. #### 2.6.7 Economic Challenges

**Problem:** Funding and financial sustainability are significant challenges for shared electric bicycle networks, particularly in the initial stages of implementation.

**Countermeasure:** Governments and public agencies should provide financial support, incentives, and subsidies to promote the development of shared electric bicycle networks. **Countermeasure:** Engaging with the community through public consultations, community outreach programs, and transparent communication can help build trust and support for shared electric bicycle networks. #### 2.6.9 Operational Challenges

**Problem:** Efficient operations and maintenance of shared electric bicycle networks are essential for providing a reliable service but can be complex and resource-intensive.

### 2.7 Steps for Constructing New Urban Shared Electric Bicycle Networks

The construction of new urban shared electric bicycle networks involves a series of well-planned and meticulously executed steps. This section will outline the key steps required to establish a successful shared electric bicycle network, including network planning, facility construction, and system operations and management.

#### 2.7.1 Network Planning

Effective network planning is the cornerstone of a successful shared electric bicycle network. This step involves several critical components:

1. **Data Collection and Analysis:** Begin by collecting relevant data on population density, transportation patterns, existing infrastructure, and user preferences. This data can be used to identify high-demand areas, potential network locations, and the optimal number of bike stations.

2. **Design of Bike Stations:** Design the layout and placement of bike stations to ensure convenience and accessibility. Consider factors such as proximity to public transit, commercial areas, residential zones, and workplaces. Each station should be equipped with sufficient bike parking spaces and charging facilities.

3. **Routing and Connectivity:** Develop a comprehensive routing plan that connects various parts of the city, ensuring that the network is both functional and user-friendly. This involves mapping out routes that are safe, efficient, and easy to navigate.

4. **Capacity Planning:** Determine the capacity of the network by estimating the number of bicycles and charging stations required based on user demand and traffic patterns. This ensures that the network can handle peak usage times without congestion or shortages.

5. **Environmental Impact Assessment:** Conduct an environmental impact assessment to evaluate the potential environmental benefits and challenges associated with the network. This includes assessing the impact on air quality, noise levels, and urban aesthetics.

#### 2.7.2 Facility Construction

Once the network planning is complete, the next step is to construct the necessary facilities:

1. **Bike Stations:** Construct bike stations at the planned locations, ensuring that each station is equipped with bike racks, locks, and charging stations. The design should be user-friendly and secure to encourage usage.

2. **Charging Infrastructure:** Install charging infrastructure at each bike station to accommodate the electric bicycles' battery needs. This may include fast-charging stations and regular charging points.

3. **Road Infrastructure:** If necessary, construct or modify road infrastructure to accommodate bike lanes and pathways. This ensures the safety and convenience of cyclists.

4. **Signage and Wayfinding:** Install clear and visible signage and wayfinding tools to help users navigate the network effectively. This includes information about station locations, routing, and bicycle usage instructions.

5. **Security Measures:** Implement security measures to protect the bikes and stations from theft and vandalism. This may include surveillance cameras, fencing, and security lighting.

#### 2.7.3 System Operations and Management

The successful operation and management of a shared electric bicycle network are crucial for ensuring its sustainability and user satisfaction:

1. **Maintenance and Repair:** Establish a regular maintenance and repair schedule for the bicycles and charging stations. This helps prevent breakdowns and ensures that the network remains reliable and functional.

2. **User Support:** Provide user support services, such as customer service hotlines, online help desks, and user manuals. This helps users resolve any issues they may encounter and ensures a smooth user experience.

3. **Data Monitoring and Analysis:** Continuously monitor the network's performance through data collection and analysis. This includes tracking usage patterns, battery status, and maintenance needs. Data-driven insights can be used to optimize operations and improve the network's efficiency.

4. **User Feedback:** Gather user feedback through surveys, reviews, and feedback forms. This helps identify areas for improvement and ensures that the network meets the needs and expectations of its users.

5. **Marketing and Promotion:** Develop and implement marketing campaigns to promote the shared electric bicycle network to potential users. This includes public relations efforts, social media campaigns, and partnerships with local businesses and community organizations.

#### 2.7.4 Continuous Improvement

The final step in constructing a shared electric bicycle network is to establish a process for continuous improvement:

1. **Regular Evaluation:** Conduct regular evaluations of the network's performance, user satisfaction, and operational efficiency. This helps identify areas that need improvement and informs decision-making.

2. **Innovation and Adaptation:** Encourage innovation and adaptation to new technologies and best practices. This ensures that the network remains cutting-edge and responsive to the changing needs of urban mobility.

3. **Collaboration and Partnerships:** Foster collaboration with government agencies, private sector partners, and community organizations. This helps leverage resources, expertise, and support to enhance the network's sustainability and impact.

#### 2.7.5 Conclusion

Constructing a new urban shared electric bicycle network requires careful planning, strategic implementation, and ongoing management. By following these steps and addressing the unique challenges of each phase, cities can establish a sustainable and effective shared electric bicycle network that promotes green mobility and enhances urban quality of life. ### 2.8 User Experience and Satisfaction Assessment

4. **Reliability:** Evaluate the reliability of the service. This includes the availability of functional bicycles and charging stations, the speed of response to maintenance issues, and the accuracy of GPS tracking.

5. **Affordability:** Examine the cost-effectiveness of the shared electric bicycle service. Assess whether the pricing structure is reasonable for different user groups and whether it discourages excessive use.
