## The Dynamics of Cultural Integration

### Core Concepts and Relationships

#### Table 3: Core Concepts of Cultural Integration

| Concept         | Definition                                       | Influence                  |
|-----------------|--------------------------------------------------|---------------------------|
| Cultural Exchange | The exchange of ideas, beliefs, and practices between cultures. | Key driver of cultural integration. |
| Assimilation     | The process by which a smaller culture adopts the characteristics of a dominant culture. | Can lead to loss of cultural identity. |
| Acculturation    | The process of cultural change in a society or group of people under the influence of another culture. | Involves both adaptation and resistance. |
| Syncretism       | The blending of different cultural elements to create a new, hybrid culture. | Fosters diversity and innovation. |
