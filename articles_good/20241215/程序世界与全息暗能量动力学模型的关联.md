$$
f(x) = \frac{x+1}{x^2} = \frac{x(x+1)}{x^2(x+1)} = \frac{x^2+x}{x^3+x^2}
$$

最后，化简分数：

$$
f(x) = \frac{x^2+x}{x^3+x^2} = \frac{x(x+1)}{x^2(x+1)} = \frac{1}{x}
$$
