### Core Leadership Theories

Leadership theories provide frameworks for understanding the various aspects of leadership and how leaders can effectively influence their teams. There are several key theories that have shaped our understanding of leadership, each offering unique insights into different leadership styles and approaches. In this section, we will explore three prominent leadership theories: transformational vs. transactional leadership, situational leadership theory, and servant leadership.

#### Transformational vs. Transactional Leadership

Transformational leadership is characterized by the ability of a leader to inspire and motivate their team members to achieve their full potential. Transformational leaders focus on creating a vision for the future, articulating this vision to their team, and then driving the necessary changes to make it a reality. They are known for their ability to inspire and motivate their team through positive reinforcement, recognizing and rewarding individual and team achievements, and fostering a culture of innovation and continuous improvement.

Transformational leadership is often associated with charismatic leaders who have a strong vision and the ability to communicate this vision effectively. They set high expectations for their team members and empower them to reach these goals. Transformational leaders also demonstrate emotional intelligence, displaying empathy and understanding towards their team members, and building strong relationships based on trust and respect.

On the other hand, transactional leadership is more focused on maintaining order and ensuring that tasks are completed efficiently. Transactional leaders rely on established systems and processes to manage their teams, using rewards and punishments to motivate their team members to meet organizational goals. They are effective at coordinating and organizing resources, ensuring that tasks are completed on time and within budget.

While transactional leadership is essential in maintaining stability and efficiency within an organization, transformational leadership is often seen as more effective in driving innovation and achieving long-term success. Transformational leaders inspire their team members to go above and beyond, fostering a sense of ownership and commitment to the organization's goals. They create a positive work environment that encourages collaboration, creativity, and personal growth.

#### Situational Leadership Theory

Situational leadership theory posits that there is no one-size-fits-all approach to leadership. Instead, effective leaders must be able to adapt their leadership style to the specific situation and the needs of their team members. This theory was developed by Paul Hersey and Ken Blanchard and is based on the idea that leaders should use different styles of leadership depending on the readiness level of their followers.

The readiness level of a follower is determined by their ability and willingness to perform a specific task. There are four levels of readiness:

1. **Level 1: Low Ability, Low Willingness**: Followers need both guidance and support to perform the task.
2. **Level 2: Low Ability, High Willingness**: Followers are eager to perform the task but lack the necessary skills or knowledge.
3. **Level 3: High Ability, Low Willingness**: Followers have the skills to perform the task but may lack motivation or confidence.
4. **Level 4: High Ability, High Willingness**: Followers are fully capable and willing to perform the task independently.

Based on the readiness level of their followers, situational leadership theory suggests that leaders should use one of four different leadership styles:

1. **Directing**: For followers at Level 1, leaders should provide clear instructions and closely supervise their work.
2. **Coaching**: For followers at Level 2, leaders should provide guidance and support while allowing them to take on more responsibility.
3. **Supporting**: For followers at Level 3, leaders should offer encouragement and autonomy, allowing them to take charge of their tasks.
4. **Delegating**: For followers at Level 4, leaders should delegate tasks and trust their followers to complete them independently.

Situational leadership theory emphasizes the importance of flexibility and adaptability in leadership. Effective leaders are those who can assess the readiness level of their followers and adjust their leadership style accordingly. This approach allows leaders to build strong relationships with their team members and tailor their leadership strategies to the specific needs of each individual.

#### Servant Leadership

Servant leadership is a philosophy where the primary focus of a leader is to serve the needs of their team members and the organization as a whole. This approach is rooted in the belief that true leadership arises from a desire to serve and help others succeed. Servant leaders prioritize the well-being and development of their team members, recognizing that the success of the organization depends on the growth and satisfaction of its people.

Servant leaders exhibit several key characteristics, including:

1. **Empathy**: They are able to understand and share the feelings of their team members, showing genuine concern for their well-being.
2. **Listening**: They actively listen to their team members, valuing their input and incorporating it into decision-making processes.
3. **Humility**: They are humble and do not seek personal glory, focusing instead on the success of their team.
4. **Responsiveness**: They are quick to respond to the needs of their team members and the organization, taking action to address any issues or challenges.
5. **Visionary**: They have a clear vision for the future and work to align their team's efforts with this vision.

Servant leadership is particularly effective in creating a positive work environment that fosters trust, collaboration, and innovation. By prioritizing the needs of their team members, servant leaders build strong relationships and empower their team to reach their full potential. This approach not only improves individual performance but also drives overall organizational success.

In conclusion, the core leadership theories of transformational vs. transactional leadership, situational leadership theory, and servant leadership provide valuable insights into different approaches to leadership. Each theory offers unique perspectives on how leaders can effectively inspire and motivate their teams. By understanding and applying these theories, leaders can adapt their approach to the specific needs of their team and create a positive, productive work environment.

        user = User.query.filter_by(username=form.username.data).first()
        if user and user.check_password(form.password.data):
            return redirect(url_for('main.home'))
        else:
            flash('Invalid username or password')
    return render_template('login.html', form=form)

@bp.route('/register', methods=['GET', 'POST'])
def register():
    form = RegistrationForm()
    if form.validate_on_submit():
        new_user = User(
            username=form.username.data,
            password=form.password.data,
            is_leader=form.is_leader.data,
            is_trainer=form.is_trainer.data
        )
        db.session.add(new_user)
        db.session.commit()
        return redirect(url_for('main.login'))
    return render_template('register.html', form=form)
